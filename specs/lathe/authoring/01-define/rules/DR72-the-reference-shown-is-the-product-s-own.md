---
id: LA-AUT-DR72
kind: domain_rule
title: The reference shown is the product's own
parent: LA-AUT
---
The kinds, relations, folders and checks a format page shows MUST be the product's own: the meta-model after its extensions, and Lathe's checks with the product's. When the engine does not serve the reference, the pages MUST show what the loaded specs tell (their kinds, relations, folders and the checks that report problems) and say that the explanations are missing.
