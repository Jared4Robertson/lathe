---
id: LA-AUT-DR55
kind: domain_rule
title: A reference may cross aggregates and domains
parent: LA-AUT-EN08
---
A `refers_to` link MAY join an entity to an entity of any aggregate of its product, in its own domain or another; it is the only entity link that may leave the aggregate, and like every link it stays inside its product (`LA-AUT-DR75`).
