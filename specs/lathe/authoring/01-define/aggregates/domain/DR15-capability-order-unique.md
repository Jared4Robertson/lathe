---
id: LA-AUT-DR15
kind: domain_rule
title: Each capability in a domain has its own whole-number order
parent: LA-AUT-EN05
---
A capability's `order` MUST be a whole number, and no two capabilities in the same domain MAY have the same order read as a number (`1` and `01` are the same). The order sets the capability's place in the domain, and the domain's use case index lists capabilities in that order.
