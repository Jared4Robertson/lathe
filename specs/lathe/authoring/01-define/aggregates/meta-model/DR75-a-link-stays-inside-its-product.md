---
id: LA-AUT-DR75
kind: domain_rule
title: A link stays inside its product
parent: LA-AUT-EN01
---
Each product is a space of its own: every link in a record's `links` (`satisfies`, `involves`, `applies` or any other relation) MUST target a record of the same product. A citation of another product's ID in prose is not a link and is allowed; it is not a mention either, and reads as plain text.
