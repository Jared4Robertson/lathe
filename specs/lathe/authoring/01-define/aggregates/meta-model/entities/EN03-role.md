One role the product's use cases name as their actor, such as Owner, Reader or Agent. Specs name roles, never people.

## Fields

| Field | Meaning | Kind of value | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Name | The role, as an actor names it. | Text, one word. | Librarian |
| What it does | What a person or tool in this role does with the product, said in the product's body. | Text. | Lends tools and records their return. |
