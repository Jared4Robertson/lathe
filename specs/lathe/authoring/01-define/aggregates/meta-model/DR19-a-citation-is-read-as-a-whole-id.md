---
id: LA-AUT-DR19
kind: domain_rule
title: A citation is read as a whole ID
parent: LA-AUT-EN01
---
A citation MUST be an ID-shaped token read whole, and is one only under LA-AUT-DR27 to LA-AUT-DR29. A token is never split: the tail of `X-TL-LEND-DR05` is not a citation of `TL-LEND-DR05`, and `TL-LEND-DR05a` cites nothing.
