---
id: LA-AUT-DR27
kind: domain_rule
title: A token that names a record is a citation
parent: LA-AUT-EN01
---
An ID-shaped token in a body that is the ID of a record MUST be a citation, whatever its prefix, so a product whose IDs do not start with its own ID (such as `ACC-UC01` and `STD-04`) still has its citations read.
