---
id: LA-AUT-DR78
kind: domain_rule
title: Each step of a technical flow has its own place on its path
parent: LA-AUT-EN27
---
The steps on each path of a technical flow MUST each have a different whole-number `order`, compared as numbers, numbered 1, 2, 3 without gaps, as a flow's steps are. A step with no path MUST be on the main path.
