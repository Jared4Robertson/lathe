---
id: LA-AUT-DR77
kind: domain_rule
title: A standard rule is in use only when something meets or cites it
parent: LA-AUT-A12
---
A standard rule MUST count as in use when a record links to it with `meets`, or when a record of its product other than the rule itself and its own standard cites its ID in prose. A rule that is not in use has no purpose and MUST be reported.
