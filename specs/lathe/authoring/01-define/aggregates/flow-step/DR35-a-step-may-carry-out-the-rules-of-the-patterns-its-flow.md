---
id: LA-AUT-DR35
kind: domain_rule
title: A step may carry out the rules of the patterns its flow follows
parent: LA-AUT-EN19
---
A pattern rule MUST count as inside a flow when its pattern is followed by a use case the flow carries out or by the flow's domain, and so MUST an interaction requirement that fulfills such a rule. A step that fulfills any other pattern rule MUST be reported as outside its flow.
