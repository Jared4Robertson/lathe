kind: requirement
title: Requirements, interaction requirements, pattern rules and standard rules may name a group
parent: LA-AUT-UC13
---
A `requirement`, `interaction_requirement`, `pattern_rule` or `standard_rule` MAY name a short heading in `group`, such as Validation or Paging, so that a long list of them is sorted under headings as a real product sorts it. Groups MUST be compared trimmed and in any letter case. A group is a field, not a record, so renaming one MUST change no ID.
