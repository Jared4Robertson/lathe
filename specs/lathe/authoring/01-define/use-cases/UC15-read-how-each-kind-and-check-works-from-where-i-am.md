The Reader meets a kind or a check while reading the spec (a record's kind in the side bar, a problem's code, a required field) and opens what the format says about it in one step, without leaving the workbench. Each kind and each check has its own page, generated from the meta-model and the check files, so it is never out of date: a kind's place, fields, links and checks with a real example from the product, and a check's explanation with the problems it reports now. The record format's own page gives the concepts and an index of everything.
