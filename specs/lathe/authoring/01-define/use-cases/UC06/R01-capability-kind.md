id: LA-AUT-UC06-R01
kind: requirement
title: A capability sits under a domain with a whole-number order
parent: LA-AUT-UC06
---
A `capability` record MUST sit under a `domain` and MUST give its place in the domain in a required `order` field. A `use_case`, `flow` or `test` MAY sit under either a domain or one of its capabilities.
