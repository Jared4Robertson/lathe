id: LA-AUT-UC14-R05
kind: requirement
title: A domain rule may sit under an entity, and a layer under a layer
parent: LA-AUT-UC14
---
A domain rule MAY sit under an entity, and a layer MAY sit under another layer. Coverage, the structure checks and every check that reads a record's domain MUST treat these records as they treat the same records one level up.
