id: LA-AUT-UC14-R04
kind: requirement
title: Format pages open in the workbench, with the side bar showing the facts
parent: LA-AUT-UC14
---
Each format page MUST open as any other page does: one pane with the page's title in it, and the side bar beside it (a bottom sheet on a phone) showing the kind's or the check's facts, shown and hidden with the same toggle and command as on a record page.
