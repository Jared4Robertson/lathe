title: A check page shows its explanation and the problems it reports now
parent: LA-AUT-UC14
---
A check's page (`/format/checks/<check>`) MUST show its severity, its summary and explanation (rendered Markdown), where it comes from and its source file, the kinds it concerns, and the current space's problems with that code as clickable rows.
