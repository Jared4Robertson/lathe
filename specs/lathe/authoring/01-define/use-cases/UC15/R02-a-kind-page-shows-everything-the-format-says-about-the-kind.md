id: LA-AUT-UC15-R02
kind: requirement
title: A kind page shows everything the format says about the kind, with a real example
parent: LA-AUT-UC15
---
A kind's page (`/format/kinds/<kind>`) MUST show its description; its stage and folder; its allowed parents and children as links to their kind pages; its required fields, each with its own anchor, its obligation and its ID convention; the relations out of it and into it by their readable labels, each naming the kind at the other end; and the checks that concern it. It MUST show the product's first record of the kind (the current space's first, by ID) with its header as code and a link to it, and the count of the product's records of the kind, with a row per parent leading to that parent's group of them.
