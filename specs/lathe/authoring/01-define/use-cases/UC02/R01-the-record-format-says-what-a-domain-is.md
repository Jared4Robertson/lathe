---
id: LA-AUT-UC02-R01
kind: requirement
title: The record format says what a domain is
parent: LA-AUT-UC02
---
The record format MUST say that a domain is a business capability with its own language, whose Define stage holds its domain model and use cases and whose Design stage holds its views, endpoints, components in their layers, and tests; that what every domain shares goes in a platform domain or a pattern; and it MUST give one good and one bad example, in general terms.
