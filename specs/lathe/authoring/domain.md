Writing a product down in Lathe's one language: its records and what every record shares, its domains by capability, their aggregates, use cases, patterns, layers, flows, views, endpoints, tests and open questions, and what a product adds to Lathe through its Product extension. Each of those concepts is an aggregate here, with the rules for writing it, and the format reference shows them all.

The checks that hold records to these rules are designed here, beside the rules; Checking runs them and reports what they find.
