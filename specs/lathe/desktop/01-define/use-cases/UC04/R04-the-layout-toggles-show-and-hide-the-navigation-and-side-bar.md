id: LA-DSK-UC04-R04
kind: requirement
title: The layout toggles show and hide the navigation and the side bar
parent: LA-DSK-UC04
---
The bar MUST end with toggle buttons for the navigation and the side bar that run `toggleNav` and `toggleSideBar`, the same commands as the menus and the keys. Each MUST say with `aria-pressed` whether its panel shows, and its tooltip MUST show the shortcut. The side bar toggle MUST be disabled on a page that has no side bar.
