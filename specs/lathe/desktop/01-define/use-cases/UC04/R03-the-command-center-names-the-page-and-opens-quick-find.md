title: The command center names the page and opens quick find
parent: LA-DSK-UC04
---
The bar's center MUST hold a command center: a pill showing the product name and the current page's heading ("Lathe — Add an item to the basket"), centered on the window and cut short with an ellipsis when it does not fit. Clicking it MUST open quick find (`quickOpen`), and its tooltip MUST show the shortcut.
