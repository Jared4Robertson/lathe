kind: requirement
title: The layout toggles show and hide the navigation and the inspector
parent: LA-DSK-UC04
---
The bar MUST end with toggle buttons for the navigation and the inspector that run `toggleNav` and `toggleInspector`, the same commands as the menus and the keys. Each MUST say with `aria-pressed` whether its panel shows, and its tooltip MUST show the shortcut. The inspector toggle MUST be disabled on a page that has no inspector.
