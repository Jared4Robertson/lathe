id: LA-DSK-UC05-R02
kind: requirement
title: Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab switch record tabs in the desktop app
parent: LA-DSK-UC05
---
View › Tabs MUST offer Show Next Tab, Show Previous Tab, Keep Tab Open and Close Tab, running `nextTab`, `previousTab`, `keepTab` and `closeTab` by command ID. Show Next Tab and Show Previous Tab MUST own Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab as their accelerators, since a web page never receives those keys; the other two MUST take no key from the app.
