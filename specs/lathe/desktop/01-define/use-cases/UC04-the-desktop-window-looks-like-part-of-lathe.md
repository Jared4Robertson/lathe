Jared works in a window with no system title bar, as in VS Code: the window's controls float over Lathe's own bar, drawn in the theme's colors. From the bar he goes back and forward, sees which page he is on and opens quick find, and shows or hides the navigation and the inspector. He drags the window by the bar and double-clicks it to zoom. When he changes the theme or the light or dark mode, the bar and the window's controls follow.
