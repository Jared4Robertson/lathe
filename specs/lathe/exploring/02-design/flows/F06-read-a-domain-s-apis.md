1. The reader opens a domain's Interaction design folder in the nav and sees its APIs and integrations beside its flows and views.
2. They open an API and read its endpoints, with their methods and routes, and the requirements every endpoint shares.
3. They open an integration and read each exchange with its outside system.
4. In Technical specification, they open a layer's technical flow and read its steps path by path, with the component each uses and what it satisfies.
