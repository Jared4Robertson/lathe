How the Reader uses the side bar. In the Graph view it is already open on Details: they move the pointer over the boxes and read each record's facts and traceability in turn, then open one with Open. In the Wiki view they open the side bar from the page bar, switch to Outline and jump to the Traceability section, then switch to History, see the uncommitted change at the top, open an older commit's diff, and load more. Their choices come back with the tab after a reload, while the Wiki view of another tab stays closed.
