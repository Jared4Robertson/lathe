The graph view over the one map (`LA-EXP-C64`), drawn on a canvas each frame by the painter (`LA-EXP-C80`) from each box's plate (`LA-EXP-C81`): record boxes in their stage's colors with error and gap badges, each kind drawn in its shape (`LA-EXP-DR65`, a domain a hexagon, `LA-EXP-DR61`), as its outline in the Tree view and a mark in the Nested and Voronoi views, folder-shaped groups, every drawn box with its label and bars where the level of detail asks for them (`LA-EXP-DR60`), level parent lines and dashed, curved cross-links with their words, the toolbar, filters, side bar, legend and minimap (a true overview of the map, `LA-EXP-UC03-R15`). The text measure the maps are laid out with is the canvas's, once the label's faces have loaded (`LA-EXP-C82`); each line is drawn at its map size times the zoom (`LA-EXP-DR58`), so nothing is laid out again and text is crisp at any zoom. The camera (`LA-EXP-C49`) is the page's own: a new focus, depth or pane size glides it (`LA-EXP-DR59`); the wheel and a pinch zoom about the pointer, a drag pans, the zoom buttons zoom about the middle, and each move draws the next frame, every point placed relative to the camera's center. The pointer finds the box under it by the map's outlines (`LA-EXP-C84`), for the hover spotlight (fading on the shared motion), the tooltip, a click and a tap. A visually hidden layer of the drawn boxes, in the keyboard's order, carries focus, the arrow keys, Enter and Escape and each box's role and name; the focused box is drawn with the focus ring, and the layer takes what a glide passes over once as it starts. The Layout switch (`LA-EXP-UC03-R16`, the `graphLayout` command) draws the Nested map (`LA-EXP-C67`) and the Voronoi map (`LA-EXP-C68`, once the worker has laid it out) with the same camera, canvas, keyboard, spotlight, filters, minimap and side bar: a parent drawn before its children, no parent lines or bars, a cell drawn as its polygon, and Links between the cells' labels. The canvas's element offers a test hook, `graphScene`, with what it draws (each box's place on screen and in the map, its label lines with their fonts, its badges and states, the lines and the camera), and `graphHit`, its hit test.
