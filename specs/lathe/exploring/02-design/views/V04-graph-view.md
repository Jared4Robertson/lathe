The whole model as one map, shown as the Graph view of an address and focused on that record (or a group of the graph's tree, at its address): laid out left to right in columns by depth in the parent/child tree, like a folder tree, every parent as tall as its children and every sibling in one column, children grouped by kind, each kind drawn in its own shape (a domain a hexagon). The camera frames the focus and the levels the depth asks for; a breadcrumb trail, a depth switch, a Links switch, a trace mode switch, kind and relation filters, a minimap, and a side bar for the focused record with a legend sit around it.
