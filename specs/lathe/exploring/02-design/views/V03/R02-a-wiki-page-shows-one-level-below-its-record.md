id: LA-EXP-V03-R02
kind: interaction_requirement
title: A wiki page shows one level below its record by default
parent: LA-EXP-V03
links:
  fulfills: [LA-EXP-UC05-R12]
---
A wiki page (product, domain, record or group) and the side bar MUST show by default only the records one level below the page's own record. Anything deeper MUST be folded under its row and open only when the person opens it, opens everything, or searches for something inside it.
