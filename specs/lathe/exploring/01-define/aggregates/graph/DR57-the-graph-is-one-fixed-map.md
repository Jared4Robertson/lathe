---
id: LA-EXP-DR57
kind: domain_rule
title: "The graph is one fixed map of its product's tree"
parent: LA-EXP-EN06
---
Each product MUST have a graph of its own: its tree laid out once per load of the specs, as one icicle with the product as its single root, then its Shared folder, domains, stages, folders and records and their children, as the nav's tree has them (`LA-EXP-DR45`). No node, link, hint or minimap box of another product MAY be in it; a record no product reaches is the root of a map of its own. A node's size MUST come from its subtree alone, never from what is in focus: a leaf has one fixed size, and a parent is exactly as tall as its children stacked, the gaps between them included, the stack starting at its top edge, in the tree's order. Each parent line MUST be drawn level, at the child's middle, from the parent's outline to the child's. Nothing in the map is ever laid out again or resized while the person walks it; filters hide nodes without moving the rest. A trace is a chain, not a tree, and keeps a small picture of its own, in columns of leaf-high boxes.
