---
id: LA-EXP-DR57
kind: domain_rule
title: "The graph is one fixed map of the whole tree"
parent: LA-EXP-EN06
---
The graph MUST lay out the whole tree once per load of the specs, as one icicle: the products, then their Shared folders, domains, stages, folders and records and their children, as the nav's tree has them (`LA-EXP-DR45`). A node's size MUST come from its subtree alone, never from what is in focus: a leaf has one fixed size, and a parent is exactly as tall as its children stacked, the gaps between them included, the stack starting at its top edge, in the tree's order. Each parent line MUST be drawn level, at the child's middle, from the parent's outline to the child's. Nothing in the map is ever laid out again or resized while the person walks it; filters hide nodes without moving the rest. A trace is a chain, not a tree, and keeps a small picture of its own, in columns of leaf-high boxes.
