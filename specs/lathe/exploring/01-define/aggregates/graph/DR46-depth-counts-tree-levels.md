title: Depth counts the levels the camera frames below the focus
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
Depth N MUST frame the focus, its parent at the left edge for context, and its descendants down to N levels below it, every stage, folder and group counting as a level, as in a file explorer. Depth MUST NOT add siblings or ancestors further up to the picture it frames, though the map around it stays in view. Depth stays 1 to 3, and a group at the center counts levels the same way. The side bar's lists and the summary count this framed picture.
