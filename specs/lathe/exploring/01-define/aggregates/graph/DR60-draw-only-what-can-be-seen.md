id: LA-EXP-DR60
kind: domain_rule
title: "The graph draws only what is in view and big enough to see"
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
The graph MUST draw only the nodes in view (with a tenth of the pane to spare each way) and at least 3 pixels tall on screen. Whether a node is labeled MUST be decided from its size on screen (its size in the map times the camera's zoom), and decided again as the camera settles and as the person zooms: a node MUST be labeled whenever its box has room on screen for its text at 0.6 of a leaf's size or more and is 18 pixels tall or more, and its text MUST keep 0.9 of a leaf's size on screen while the box has room for it, wrapping its title in a narrow box, rather than shrinking with the map; so at depths 2 and 3 every framed box big enough for a word shows its title. Smaller, it MUST be a plain filled box with no text, out of the keyboard order and hidden from assistive technology, its name in its tooltip. A drawn node's children too small to draw MUST be one bar in their level for each run of them. Level by level from the roots, a level that would take the drawing past 260 boxes MUST be drawn as one bar per parent instead, so a product of thousands of records stays within the performance budgets (`LA-PAT-APP-UX-R11`). Labels appear as the person zooms in. What a glide passes over MUST be drawn once, as it starts, and trimmed when it ends, so no frame of the glide draws anew.
