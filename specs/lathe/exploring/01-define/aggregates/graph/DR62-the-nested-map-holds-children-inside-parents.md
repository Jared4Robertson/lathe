id: LA-EXP-DR62
kind: domain_rule
title: "The Nested map holds each box's children inside it, every box a square"
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
In the Nested view the graph MUST lay out the same tree as the Tree view (`LA-EXP-DR45`), its product's alone with the product as its single root and nothing of another product, once per load of the specs, as nested squares built from the leaves up, so every label fits by construction. A box's level here is its height from the leaves: a box with no children is level 0, and a parent is one more than its tallest child, so every leaf record reads at leaf size wherever it sits. Every box at one level MUST have the same text scale: its typical box size (the median of the square root of its boxes' areas, weighted by area, each box sized at that scale, the two settled together as a Tree level's width and heights are) over a typical leaf's, never below the level below's (1 for the leaves) and at most three times it, settled from the leaves up and set once when the map is built (`LA-EXP-DR58`), never by the focus or the zoom. Every box MUST be a square sized to its content at its level's scale, not to the records under it: a leaf is the smallest square that holds its label (its kind and ID line and its title, wrapped on whole words), its padding included; a parent's children are packed in the tree's order in rows (each row as many as fit, a gap apart, as tall as its tallest), within a row width tried a few ways to make the parent's square smallest; and a parent is the smallest square holding its header band (its own label at its level's scale) and that block below it, inside its padding. Space the block leaves is blank. A label never shrinks below its level's scale.

A box with children MUST show its label in a header band at its top, as tall as its label at its level's scale, and hold its children below it, inside a padding and a gap apart, so no two siblings overlap and no child leaves its parent. A label's kind and ID line and its title each wrap on whole words, never inside one, and nothing is clipped: a leaf's label is centered in it, a parent's sits at the top left of its header band. Every drawn box shows its label (`LA-EXP-DR60`). A domain is a square there, with a small hexagon mark in its header (`LA-EXP-DR61` holds in the Tree view). The focus never changes the map, and filters hide boxes without moving the rest. No parent lines are drawn: holding is the parent link.
