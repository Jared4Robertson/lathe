id: LA-EXP-DR62
kind: domain_rule
title: "The Nested map holds each box's children inside it"
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
In the Nested view the graph MUST lay out the same tree as the Tree view (`LA-EXP-DR45`), its product's alone with the product as its single root and nothing of another product, once per load of the specs, as a treemap of rectangles built from the leaves up, so every label fits by construction. Every box at one nesting level MUST have the same text scale: its typical box size (the median of the square root of its boxes' areas, weighted by area, each box planned at the larger of its share by records and the least its label and children need at that scale, the two settled together as a Tree level's width and heights are) over a leaf's, never below the level below's and at most three times it, set once when the map is built (`LA-EXP-DR58`), never by the focus or the zoom. A leaf's least size MUST be what its label (its kind and ID line and its title, wrapped on whole words toward a shape about 3 wide to 2 tall) needs at its level's scale, its padding included; a parent's least size is its header, its own label at its level's scale, over the least room its children need packed, a gap apart, inside its padding. Then, from the root down, the whole map 1.6 times as wide as it is tall and at least its least size, each box's children MUST share its room, each the larger of its share by the leaf records under it and its least size, scaled so they exactly fill it: area stays in proportion to the records wherever the least sizes allow, and no box is smaller than its least size where its parent has the room. A box whose label still does not fit the cell it got, at its level's scale, MUST have its least size grown and the map laid out again, a bounded number of times; a label never shrinks below its level's scale.

A box with children MUST show its label in a header band at its top, as tall as its label at its level's scale, and hold its children below it, inside a padding and a gap apart, squarified in the tree's order (row after row along the shorter side, never sorted by size), so no two siblings overlap and no child leaves its parent. A label's kind and ID line and its title each wrap on whole words, never inside one, and nothing is clipped: a leaf's label is centered in it, a parent's sits at the top left of its header band. Every drawn box shows its label (`LA-EXP-DR60`). A domain is a rectangle there, with a small hexagon mark in its header (`LA-EXP-DR61` holds in the Tree view). The focus never changes the map, and filters hide boxes without moving the rest. No parent lines are drawn: holding is the parent link.
