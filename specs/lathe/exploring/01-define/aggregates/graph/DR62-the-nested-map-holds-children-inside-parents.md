---
id: LA-EXP-DR62
kind: domain_rule
title: "The Nested map holds each box's children inside it"
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
In the Nested view the graph MUST lay out the same tree as the Tree view (`LA-EXP-DR45`), its product's alone with the product as its single root and nothing of another product, once per load of the specs, as a treemap of rectangles built from the leaves up, so every label fits by construction. A leaf's least size MUST be what its label (its kind and ID line and its title, wrapped on whole words toward a shape about 3 wide to 2 tall) needs at text scale 1, its padding included; a parent's least size is its header band over the least room its children need packed, a gap apart, inside its padding. Then, from the root down, the whole map 1.6 times as wide as it is tall and at least its least size, each box's children MUST share its room, each the larger of its share by the leaf records under it and its least size, scaled so they exactly fill it: area stays in proportion to the records wherever the least sizes allow, and no box is smaller than its least size where its parent has the room.

A box with children MUST show its label in a header band at its top, a fifth of its size (the square root of its area) and never more than 0.4 of its height, and hold its children below it, inside a padding and a gap apart, squarified in the tree's order (row after row along the shorter side, never sorted by size), so no two siblings overlap and no child leaves its parent. Each box MUST have a text scale of its own, in proportion to it: the largest its label fits at in its box, a leaf's in its height and a parent's in its header band, set when the map is built (`LA-EXP-DR58`), never by the focus or the zoom, so a big box always has a big label. A label's kind and ID line and its title each wrap on whole words, never inside one, and nothing is clipped: a leaf's label is centered in it, a parent's sits at the top left of its header band. Every drawn box shows its label (`LA-EXP-DR60`). A domain is a rectangle there, with a small hexagon mark in its header (`LA-EXP-DR61` holds in the Tree view). The focus never changes the map, and filters hide boxes without moving the rest. No parent lines are drawn: holding is the parent link.
