---
id: LA-EXP-DR62
kind: domain_rule
title: "The Nested map holds each box's children inside it"
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
In the Nested view the graph MUST lay out the same tree as the Tree view (`LA-EXP-DR45`), its product's alone with the product as its single root and nothing of another product, once per load of the specs, as a treemap of rectangles: each box's area in proportion to the leaf records under it (a leaf counts one), the whole map 1.6 times as wide as it is tall. A box with children MUST show its label in a header band at its top and hold its children below it, inside a padding and a gap apart, squarified in the tree's order (row after row along the shorter side, never sorted by size), so no two siblings overlap and no child leaves its parent. Every box at one nesting level MUST have one text scale, fixed when the map is built (`LA-EXP-DR58`), never by the focus or the zoom. A label's kind and ID line and its title each wrap on whole words, never inside one, and nothing is clipped: a leaf's label is centered in its whole height, and a parent's sits in a header band as tall as the label, which may take at most 0.4 of the box's size (the square root of its area) over the square root of the leaf records under it, never more than 0.4 nor less than a fifth of it, and never more than 0.4 of its height, so a parent of one record has room for its name and a parent of hundreds does not crowd them. A level's scale MUST be the largest that three quarters of its boxes, each counted by its size, can hold their labels at; a box whose label does not fit at its level's scale MUST be a plain shape with no header band, its name in its tooltip. A domain is a rectangle there, with a small hexagon mark in its header (`LA-EXP-DR61` holds in the Tree view). The focus never changes the map, and filters hide boxes without moving the rest. No parent lines are drawn: holding is the parent link.
