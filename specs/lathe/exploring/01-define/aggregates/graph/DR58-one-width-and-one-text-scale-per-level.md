---
id: LA-EXP-DR58
kind: domain_rule
title: "One width and one text scale per level; titles wrap on whole words"
parent: LA-EXP-EN06
---
Every node in one level of the map MUST have the same width, in proportion to the level's typical height (its median weighted by height), never narrower than the level below and at most three times as wide, and the products' level at most one and a half times its domains' level, so the product's picture stays usable. The gaps between siblings and between levels grow with the width. Titles never set a width.

A node's label is part of the map. Every node in a level MUST have one text scale, the level's width over a leaf level's (1 for a leaf-wide level), set once when the map is built, so a parent's text is at most three times its child's. Its title, its kind and ID line, its padding and its problem badge MUST grow by that scale (`--node-scale`), in map units, whatever the person's text size; its border, outline and parent line's stroke grow by its box's scale (`--box-scale`), the smaller of its width and height over a leaf's, never below 1. The text MUST never change size relative to its box: not when the focus changes, not when the camera zooms or settles, with no compensation for its size on screen; only the camera changes how big it looks. Every drawn box shows its label at that size, however small it is on screen (`LA-EXP-DR60`). In the Nested view each box has a text scale of its own instead (`LA-EXP-DR62`).

A title MUST wrap on whole words, after a space, never cut off or ended with an ellipsis; only a word too wide for a line on its own MAY break, after a hyphen (or a slash in a file's path) first. The kind and ID line MAY wrap too. A leaf MUST be as tall as a leaf, or as its wrapped label needs at its level's text scale with its padding if that is taller; a parent stays exactly as tall as its children stacked, so a parent whose own label needs more room than its stack passes the difference down to its last leaf. Lines MUST be broken from estimated text widths (a table of character widths for the label's faces, erring a few percent wide) when the map is built, never by measuring the page, and the page MUST draw exactly those lines, so the map is laid out once per spec load. Each line's height MUST be in proportion to its text, so no glyph is clipped. A parent's label, and a leaf's in a level wider than a leaf, MUST be centered in it, horizontally and vertically.
