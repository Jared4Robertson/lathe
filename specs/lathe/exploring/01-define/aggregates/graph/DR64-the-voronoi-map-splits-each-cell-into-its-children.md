kind: domain_rule
title: "The Voronoi map splits each cell into its children's cells"
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
In the Voronoi view the graph MUST lay out the same tree as the Tree view (`LA-EXP-DR45`), its product's alone with the product as its single root, once per load of the specs, as a Voronoi treemap: the product a flat-topped regular hexagon whose area is in proportion to the leaf records under it, and each cell with children split among them by a weighted Voronoi diagram (a power diagram) clipped to the cell's outline inside a padding and below its header, its children a gap apart, every cell convex. Every cell MUST have at least the area its own name needs (`LA-PAT-APP-UX-R12`), worked out from the leaves up before any diagram is made: a leaf three times its label's box at text scale 1, wrapped toward 3 wide to 2 tall, and a parent its children's least areas with 30% to spare over what its header and padding leave them (0.65 of it), and at least three times its own label's box. Each child's weight is its share of its parent's room by leaf records (a leaf counts one), or its least area if that is more, the others scaled down to make up the difference; wherever the least areas allow, a cell's area MUST be within about 5% of its share by leaf records. The diagram is iterated until the area error is under 0.2% of the parent's area, and a weight is rounded to within 2%. The layout MUST be deterministic: a parent's children start on a circle round its largest inscribed point, in the tree's order clockwise from the upper left, each at the middle of its share of the circle by weight and nudged by its ID, with a random number generator seeded by their IDs, and each parent is laid out in its own frame, so the same tree always gives the same picture, and adding one record moves nothing outside the parents whose rounded weight it changes. A cell's text scale MUST come from its own size, the square root of its area, set when the map is built and never by the focus or the zoom; a leaf's label is centered on its largest inscribed point, as wide as the cell allows there, and a parent's sits in a header at the top of its cell, its children below the header's cut, which leaves them at least 55% of the cell. A label wraps on whole words and is never clipped: when a title is too long for its cell at the cell's scale, the scale steps down until the label fits, so every cell shows its name. A domain is a cell there, with the hexagon mark before its kind and ID line. The focus never changes the map, filters hide cells without moving the rest, and no parent lines are drawn: holding is the parent link. The layout MUST run in a web worker where there is one, and a spec loaded again with the same tree and labels MUST reuse the map it had.
