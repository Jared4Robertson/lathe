title: "The Voronoi map splits each cell into its children's cells"
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
In the Voronoi view the graph MUST lay out the same tree as the Tree view (`LA-EXP-DR45`), its product's alone with the product as its single root, once per load of the specs, as a Voronoi treemap, top-down from the product's fixed hexagon by `LA-EXP-DR58`'s rule: siblings are the same size, with the same text. The product is a flat-topped regular hexagon as wide as the other layouts' product box, and each cell with children MUST be split into equal-area cells for them, each within about 5% of the others, by a Voronoi diagram (a power diagram) clipped to the cell's outline inside a padding and below its header, its children a gap apart, every cell convex. The diagram is iterated until the area error is under 0.2% of the parent's area. Record counts size nothing. The layout MUST be deterministic: a parent's children start evenly round a circle about its largest inscribed point, in the tree's order clockwise from the upper left, each nudged by its ID, with a random number generator seeded by their IDs, and each parent is laid out in its own frame, so the same tree always gives the same picture, and adding one record moves nothing outside its parent's cell.

Siblings MUST share one text scale: their size (the square root of a cell's area) over 400 map units, or the largest below it at which every sibling's label fits its room, the smallest sibling cell's room deciding. A leaf's label is centered on its largest inscribed point, as wide as the cell allows there, and a parent's sits in a header at the top of its cell, its children below the header's cut, which leaves them at least 55% of the cell. A label wraps on whole words and is never clipped, so every cell shows its name (`LA-PAT-APP-UX-R12`). A domain is a cell there, with the hexagon mark before its kind and ID line. The focus never changes the map, filters hide cells without moving the rest, and no parent lines are drawn: holding is the parent link. The layout MUST run in a web worker where there is one, and a spec loaded again with the same tree and labels MUST reuse the map it had.
