id: LA-EXP-DR64
kind: domain_rule
title: "The Voronoi map splits each cell into its children's cells"
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
In the Voronoi view the graph MUST lay out the same tree as the Tree view (`LA-EXP-DR45`), its product's alone with the product as its single root, once per load of the specs, as a Voronoi treemap: the product a flat-topped regular hexagon whose area is in proportion to the leaf records under it, and each cell with children split among them by a weighted Voronoi diagram (a power diagram) clipped to the cell's outline inside a padding and below its header, its children a gap apart, every cell convex. Every cell at one nesting level MUST have the same text scale: 1 at the deepest level under the product, and each level up 1.5 times the level below's, set when the map is built (`LA-EXP-DR58`) and never by the focus or the zoom. Each cell's weight MUST be the larger of its share of its parent by leaf records (a leaf counts one) and the area its name needs at its level's scale in a cell of typical shape (a parent's, its header's and its children's), so a cell's area is within about 5% of its share by records wherever its name allows. The diagram is iterated until the area error is under 0.2% of the parent's area, and a weight is rounded to within 2%. The layout MUST be deterministic: a parent's children start on a circle round its largest inscribed point, in the tree's order clockwise from the upper left, each at the middle of its share of the circle by weight and nudged by its ID, with a random number generator seeded by their IDs, and each parent is laid out in its own frame, so the same tree always gives the same picture, and adding one record moves nothing outside the parents whose rounded weight it changes. A leaf's label is centered on its largest inscribed point, as wide as the cell allows there, and a parent's sits in a header at the top of its cell, its children below the header's cut, which leaves them at least 55% of the cell. A label wraps on whole words and is never clipped, nor shrunk below its level's scale: a cell whose label still does not fit the cell it got MUST have its weight grown and the map laid out again, a bounded number of times, so every cell shows its name (`LA-PAT-APP-UX-R12`). A domain is a cell there, with the hexagon mark before its kind and ID line. The focus never changes the map, filters hide cells without moving the rest, and no parent lines are drawn: holding is the parent link. The layout MUST run in a web worker where there is one, and a spec loaded again with the same tree and labels MUST reuse the map it had.
