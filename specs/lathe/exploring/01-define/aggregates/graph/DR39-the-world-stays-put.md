---
id: LA-EXP-DR39
kind: domain_rule
title: A re-center keeps the new focus where it was
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
When the graph re-centers on a record or group that was in the last picture, the whole new layout MUST be shifted so that record keeps its place in the world: its position in the new picture equals its position in the last one, and every other position moves by the same offset. A focus that was not in the last picture is laid out at the origin. The same graph and anchor MUST always give the same positions, so drawing one picture twice never moves a node.
