---
id: LA-EXP-DR40
kind: domain_rule
title: The camera frames the new focus from wherever the view is
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
After a re-center the view MUST frame the new focus: zoomed so the whole picture fits the canvas (never past full size), with the focus at the center as far as that keeps the whole picture in view. The view MUST move there from where it is, including a view the person zoomed or panned by hand, never snapping back first, with the shared motion (`--motion-speed` and `--motion-ease`); on a page's first picture and under reduced motion it MUST go there at once.
