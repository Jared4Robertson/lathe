---
id: LA-EXP-DR59
kind: domain_rule
title: "Focusing moves the camera, never the map"
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
Focusing a node, by a click, the address, Back and Forward, the step keys or the trail, MUST move the camera only: it MUST frame the focus and the levels the depth shows below it to fill the pane, never zoomed in past a leaf's own size, with the parent's level kept in view in a strip at the left edge. The framed levels MUST fit the pane, however small their labels then are on screen; the depth decides only what is framed. The camera MUST move there from the view as it is, including one the person zoomed or panned, in one smooth zoom and pan (zooming out only as far as the pan needs) on the shared motion (`--motion-speed`, `--motion-ease`, at most twice the speed for a long path); on a page's first picture, on a resize and under reduced motion it MUST go there at once. Free zoom and pan move the same camera: the wheel and a pinch zoom about the pointer, a drag pans, and the zoom buttons zoom about the pane's middle; a gesture during a glide takes over from it, and the frame button and the `.` command frame the focus again. The camera MUST zoom out far enough for the whole of any map and in far enough to frame and read the deepest leaf; no zoom limit exists for the text's sake.

The map MUST be drawn on a canvas, anew for each frame of the camera: every point placed by the camera relative to its center in double precision, so a box deep in a large map is as steady at a deep zoom as at the top, and each label's lines drawn at their size in the map times the zoom, so text is crisp at any zoom whatever the map's range. A visually hidden layer of the drawn boxes, in the keyboard's order, MUST carry focus, the arrow keys, Enter and Escape, and each box's role and name for assistive technology, and the box it focuses is drawn with the focus ring. The pointer MUST find the box under it from the map's own outlines (a box's rectangle, a shape's polygon or rounded corners, a Voronoi cell's polygon), the last drawn first, so a child drawn over its parent is found before it.
