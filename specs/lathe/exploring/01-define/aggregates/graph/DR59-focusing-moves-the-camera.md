---
id: LA-EXP-DR59
kind: domain_rule
title: "Focusing moves the camera, never the map"
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
Focusing a node, by a click, the address, Back and Forward, the step keys or the trail, MUST move the camera only: it MUST frame the focus and the levels the depth shows below it to fill the pane, with the parent's level kept in view in a strip at the left edge. The camera's zoom limits MUST let it frame the deepest leaf of a large product, which is very small against the product's box (`LA-EXP-DR58`), and fit the whole map in a phone's pane: 64 times the map's units at most, a ten-thousandth at least. A viewport MUST be kept in doubles, and a map point's place on screen worked out in doubles relative to the camera, so a deep zoom does not jitter. The framed levels MUST fit the pane, however small their labels then are on screen; the depth decides only what is framed. The camera MUST move there from the view as it is, including one the person zoomed or panned, in one smooth zoom and pan (zooming out only as far as the pan needs) on the shared motion (`--motion-speed`, `--motion-ease`, at most twice the speed for a long path); on a page's first picture, on a resize and under reduced motion it MUST go there at once. Free zoom and pan (wheel, pinch, drag, the zoom buttons) move the same camera, a gesture during a glide takes over from it, and the frame button and the `.` command frame the focus again.
