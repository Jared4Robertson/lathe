---
id: LA-EXP-DR07
kind: domain_rule
title: Whether a record's children are grouped depends on its children alone
parent: LA-EXP-EN07
---
A record's children MUST be grouped exactly when they include more than one kind (a folder only tells kinds apart; LA-EXP-DR27), whatever the focus, depth or filters, so a record always looks the same wherever it is met in the graph.
