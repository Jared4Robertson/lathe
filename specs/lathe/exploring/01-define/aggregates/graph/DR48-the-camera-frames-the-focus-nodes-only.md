---
id: LA-EXP-DR48
kind: domain_rule
title: The Model view's camera frames its boxes in focus only
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
The Model view's camera, framing a new picture and on the Fit command, MUST fit the bounding box of its boxes in focus only, so its context ring (`LA-EXP-UC11-R06`) may run past the canvas's edges; a soft vignette of the canvas's own background at its edges MUST make the cut-off read as meant.
