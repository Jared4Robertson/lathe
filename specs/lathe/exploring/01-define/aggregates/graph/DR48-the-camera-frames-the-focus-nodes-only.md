title: The camera frames the focus nodes only
parent: LA-EXP-A4
---
Framing a new picture, and the Fit command, MUST fit the bounding box of the focus nodes only, so the context ring may run past the canvas's edges; a soft vignette of the canvas's own background at its edges MUST make the cut-off read as meant.
