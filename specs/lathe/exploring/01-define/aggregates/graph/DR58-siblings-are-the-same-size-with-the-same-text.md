---
id: LA-EXP-DR58
kind: domain_rule
title: "Siblings are the same size, with the same text"
parent: LA-EXP-EN06
---
One rule MUST size every layout of the graph (Tree, Nested and Voronoi), top-down from one fixed product box: siblings are the same size, with the same text. The product's box MUST have a fixed size, the same for every product (300,000 map units), and each parent MUST split its room equally among its children, in the tree's order. Record counts and titles MUST size no box: a busy sibling packs its children smaller, and a quiet one has blank space. How each layout splits its room is `LA-EXP-DR57` (Tree), `LA-EXP-DR62` (Nested) and `LA-EXP-DR64` (Voronoi).

A node's label is part of the map. A box's text scale MUST be at most a fixed share of its box's size, the same share at every depth: its size over 400 map units, its size being its column's width in the Tree (where siblings split their parent's height), its side in Nested and the square root of its area in Voronoi. Siblings MUST share one text scale: the largest, at most that share, at which every sibling's whole label fits its room, its kind and ID line and its title wrapped on whole words, with its padding, its marks, its shape's room and its border. So a framed box's header reads at about the same size on screen whichever box is framed, and very small boxes have very small text, read by zooming in. Its title, its kind and ID line, its padding and its problem badge MUST grow by that scale (`--node-scale`), in map units, whatever the person's text size; its border, outline and parent line's stroke grow with it too (`--box-scale`, never below half in the Nested and Voronoi views), so a box's edge stays in proportion to its label; a trace's boxes are at text scale 1. The text MUST never change size relative to its box: not when the focus changes, not when the camera zooms or settles; only the camera changes how big it looks. Every drawn box shows its label at that size, however small it is on screen (`LA-EXP-DR60`, `LA-PAT-APP-UX-R12`).

A title MUST wrap on whole words, after a space, never cut off or ended with an ellipsis; only a word too wide for a line on its own MAY break, after a hyphen (or a slash in a file's path) first, and the text scale never lets that happen in a map. The kind and ID line MAY wrap too. Lines MUST be broken from estimated text widths (a table of character widths for the label's faces, erring a few percent wide) when the map is built, never by measuring the page, and the page MUST draw exactly those lines, so the map is laid out once per spec load. Each line's height MUST be in proportion to its text, so no glyph is clipped. Map units MUST be kept in doubles, never rounded, so the deepest boxes keep their places however small they are against the product's box.
