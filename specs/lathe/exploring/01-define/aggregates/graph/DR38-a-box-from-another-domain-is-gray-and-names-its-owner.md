---
id: LA-EXP-DR38
kind: domain_rule
title: A box from another domain is gray and names its owner
parent: LA-EXP-EN06
---
In an ERD a box owned by the current domain MUST be blue, and a box owned by another domain MUST be gray and labeled with its owner in parentheses: its domain for an aggregate, its domain and aggregate for an entity. A box from another aggregate of the same domain stays blue and names its aggregate.
