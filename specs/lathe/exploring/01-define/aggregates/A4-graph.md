The drawing of the model: the whole tree, the nav's, laid out once as one fixed map in which every node has one place and one size, the groups of children of one kind, the columns they sit in, and the camera over it. Focusing a node moves the camera to frame it and the levels the depth asks for, its parent kept at the left edge; only what is in view and big enough to see is drawn, with the center's own links when asked for, and filters hide some nodes without moving the rest. The graph is a view of a page, never a place of its own.
