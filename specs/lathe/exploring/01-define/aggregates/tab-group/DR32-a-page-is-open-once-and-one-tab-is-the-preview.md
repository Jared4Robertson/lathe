---
id: LA-EXP-DR32
kind: domain_rule
title: One tab per page at most, and one preview tab at most
parent: LA-EXP-A5
---
A tab group MUST NOT hold two tabs for one page, nor more than one preview tab. Opening a page already open activates its tab; a plain open of another page replaces the preview tab's page, keeping its place in the row.
