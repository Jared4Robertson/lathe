---
id: LA-EXP-DR41
kind: domain_rule
title: A selected row is the whole row
parent: LA-EXP-A3
---
In the nav, as in VS Code's explorer, a row MUST be as wide as the nav panel, indented by padding for its depth rather than by nested margins, with its indent guides and its fold chevron inside it. The current page's row MUST carry the selection over the whole row, from the panel's left edge over the indent and the chevron to its right edge, in the selection tint with its text and chevron in steel, with no bar of its own at its edge. A hovered row MUST take the hover tint over the whole row, and while the nav is used from the keyboard the selected and the focused row MUST show a 1 px steel outline. The lists stay nested for screen readers.
