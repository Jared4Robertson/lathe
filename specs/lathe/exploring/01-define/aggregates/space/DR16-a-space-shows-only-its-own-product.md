---
id: LA-EXP-DR16
kind: domain_rule
title: A space shows only its own product
parent: LA-EXP-A2
---
Every view and count inside a space MUST show only its own product's records, links, coverage and problems, and those that belong to no product: never a record, link, mention or problem of another product. Another product MUST stay one choice away in the rail.
