---
id: LA-EXP-DR13
kind: domain_rule
title: A space's icon is its own or its monogram
parent: LA-EXP-A2
---
A product's icon MUST be its `icon` field when that is one emoji or up to two characters, and otherwise its ID as a monogram. Its color MUST be its `color` field when that is a hex color or a color name, and otherwise one derived from its ID, the same on every visit.
