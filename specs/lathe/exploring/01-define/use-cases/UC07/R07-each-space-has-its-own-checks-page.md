title: Each space has its own Checks page
parent: LA-EXP-UC07
group: What a space shows
---
Each space MUST have its own Checks page, inside the space at its product's ID (`/<product-id>/checks`), opened from the nav's Tools and by the Checks shortcuts; there MUST be no Checks page for every product at once.
