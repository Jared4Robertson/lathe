---
id: LA-EXP-UC07-R09
kind: requirement
title: No view in a space shows another product
parent: LA-EXP-UC07
group: What a space shows
---
Inside a space the side bar (Details, Outline, History), the graph and its trail and minimap, the Model view, hover cards, the format reference's records and its "where the X are" links, saved tabs and the space's last page MUST show only that product, and the desktop Dock badge MUST count only its problems and the workspace's. A link into another product MUST NOT be drawn or listed, and a citation of another product's ID in prose MUST show as plain text, not as a link.
