title: Layers and test groups fold in the nav, with counts
parent: LA-EXP-UC09
---
In the side nav, layers and test groups MUST be foldable rows with their counts, one level shown by default, and the current page's branch MUST open itself through its layer or its test groups. A layer's row MUST link to the layer's page; a test group's row MUST only fold. What is folded MUST last for the session.
