kind: requirement
title: The nav collapses to a rail, and is a full-screen sheet on a narrow screen
parent: LA-EXP-UC05
---
A toggle MUST collapse the nav to a narrow rail of icons (the logo, Quick find, Diagrams, Specs, Checks), each with a tooltip and an accessible name, and expand it again; the state MUST be remembered. Under about 900 px wide the nav MUST become a full-screen sheet opened from the menu button in the top bar, with focus moved into it and kept inside it, and closed by Escape, its close button or choosing a page, with focus returned to the menu button.
