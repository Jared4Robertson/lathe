id: LA-EXP-UC05-R14
kind: requirement
title: Grouped requirements and rules read under their group headings
parent: LA-EXP-UC05
group: Lists
---
The wiki MUST show a record's children that name a group, such as a use case's requirements, a view's interaction requirements or a pattern's rules, under their group headings: the ungrouped ones first, without a heading, then each group in the order it first appears. A use case's requirement table MUST head each group's rows with its name, and the side bar's Outline MUST list the groups.
