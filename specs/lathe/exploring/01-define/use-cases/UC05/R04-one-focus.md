title: Both panes follow one focus, and Back and Forward move both
parent: LA-EXP-UC05
group: Views and panes
---
Clicking a record in the graph MUST change the address, so the wiki pane and the side bar follow; following a link in the wiki MUST re-center the graph. Back and Forward MUST move both panes. The graph's depth, filters, open groups and trace mode MUST survive moving between pages and switching views.
