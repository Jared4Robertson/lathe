---
id: LA-EXP-UC05-R11
kind: requirement
title: The side bar folds away
parent: LA-EXP-UC05
---
A record, group, product or domain page MUST have three columns: the nav, the main view and the side bar, with the views Details, Outline and History (`LA-EXP-UC12`). One side bar MUST serve the page in every view, and show nothing the main view already shows beside it (`LA-EXP-DR55`). It MUST fold away with a toggle in the page bar and a shortcut, remember that per view mode and per tab (`LA-EXP-UC12-R02`), give its space to the main view, and on a narrow screen open as a bottom sheet that takes focus, keeps it inside, and closes with Escape (`LA-PLT-UC07-R04`).
