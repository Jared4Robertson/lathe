id: LA-EXP-UC02-R05
kind: requirement
title: Anywhere on a record's row opens that record
parent: LA-EXP-UC02
---
In every list of records (the rows of a product, domain, record or group page, the side bar's children and members, coverage gaps, open questions, what a requirement fulfills, the patterns a domain follows, the fitness history, and the problems on the checks page), a click anywhere on a row MUST open the record the row leads to, not only a click on its ID or title. Cmd/Ctrl-click and middle-click MUST open it in a new tab. The row's other controls (its fold chevron, other record links, badges and buttons) MUST keep doing what they do. Keyboard focus stays on the row's one link, and its focus ring and hover cover the whole row; a tree's arrow keys work as before.
