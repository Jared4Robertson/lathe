id: LA-EXP-UC04-R08
kind: requirement
title: A parent is as tall as its children, so the tree reads like an icicle
parent: LA-EXP-UC04
group: Layout
---
In the map, a record, stage, folder or group MUST be as tall as its children stacked, each an equal part of it, and each child MUST sit right beside it, joined by a short, level parent line, so the tree reads like an icicle and no lines fan out from a parent to its children (see LA-EXP-DR57). Every node in a depth column MUST share the column's width, a fixed fraction of the map that shrinks toward the leaves, and siblings MUST share one text size, in proportion to their column's width or as large as every sibling's whole label allows, centered, the title wrapped on whole words (see LA-EXP-DR58). No node's size ever depends on what is in focus.
