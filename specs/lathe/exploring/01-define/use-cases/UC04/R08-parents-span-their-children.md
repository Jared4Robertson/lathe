id: LA-EXP-UC04-R08
kind: requirement
title: A parent is as tall as its children, so the tree reads like an icicle
parent: LA-EXP-UC04
---
In the map, a record, stage, folder or group MUST be as tall as its children stacked, and each child MUST sit right beside it, joined by a short, level parent line, so the tree reads like an icicle and no lines fan out from a parent to its children (see LA-EXP-DR57). Every node in a level MUST share the level's width, which shrinks toward the leaves with the level's typical height, and a node's title, kind, ID and chevron MUST grow with it, centered in the node (see LA-EXP-DR58). No node's size ever depends on what is in focus.
