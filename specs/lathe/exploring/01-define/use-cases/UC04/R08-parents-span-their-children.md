id: LA-EXP-UC04-R08
kind: requirement
title: A parent is as tall as its children, so the tree reads like an icicle
parent: LA-EXP-UC04
---
In the hierarchy, a record, stage, folder or group whose children are drawn in the next column MUST be as tall as those children stacked, and each child MUST sit right beside it, joined by a short, level parent line, so the tree reads like an icicle and no lines fan out from a parent to its children (see LA-EXP-DR53). Each level MUST be as wide as its tallest node allows, up to a cap, and a node's title, kind, ID and chevron MUST grow with it, centered in the node (see LA-EXP-DR54). Re-centering MUST glide each node's width and height as it glides its place.
