id: LA-EXP-UC04-R02
kind: requirement
title: A group shows its name and count, and clicking it goes to its address
parent: LA-EXP-UC04
---
A group MUST show its name and count. Every member is always in the map, so there is nothing to open or fold in place, and a group has no chevron or other control of its own. Clicking the group, or pressing Enter on it, MUST go to its address, as clicking a record does.
