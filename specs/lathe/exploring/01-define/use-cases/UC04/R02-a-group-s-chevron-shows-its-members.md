---
id: LA-EXP-UC04-R02
kind: requirement
title: A group's chevron shows whether its members are drawn
parent: LA-EXP-UC04
---
A group MUST show its count, and end in a chevron, pointing down while its members are drawn beside it and right while they are not (too small at this zoom, or out of view). The chevron is a mark, not a control: every member is always in the map, so there is nothing to open or fold in place. Clicking the group, or pressing Enter on it, MUST go to its address, as clicking a record does.
