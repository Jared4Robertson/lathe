title: Read a flow step by step
parent: LA-EXP-CAP01
actor: Reader
---
The reader opens a flow and reads its steps in order as a table, as a real product's flow tables read: what happens at each step, the view, endpoint or component it goes through, and what it carries out. They unfold a step to read more. In the graph, the steps sit under their flow, and a requirement's trace shows the step that carries it out.
