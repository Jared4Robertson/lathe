id: LA-EXP-UC12-R01
kind: requirement
title: One side bar with three views, chosen from a switcher at its top
parent: LA-EXP-UC12
---
The page's last column MUST be one side bar with a switcher at its top of three icon buttons, Details, Outline and History, each with `aria-pressed` and a tooltip, in a group labeled "Side bar views". The view shown MUST have a heading naming it and be the region the switcher's buttons control. Switching MUST keep keyboard focus on the button pressed and announce the view. The side bar keeps its width handle, its toggle in the page bar and its shortcut (`LA-EXP-UC05-R11`).
