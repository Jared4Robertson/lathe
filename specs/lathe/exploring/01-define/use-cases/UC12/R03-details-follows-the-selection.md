id: LA-EXP-UC12-R03
kind: requirement
title: Details follows the pointer and the selection, and repeats nothing the page shows
parent: LA-EXP-UC12
---
In the Graph and Model views Details MUST show the node under the pointer or keyboard focus (the graph's spotlight), else the one selected by a tap, else the page's record: its kind, title and ID, its header fields, spec file and parent, its problems, and a compact Traceability in the relations' labels (`LA-EXP-V03-R07`), with Open for any other record and, in the desktop app, Reveal and Open in editor. In the Graph view it also lists what the graph connects to the center. In the Wiki view Details MUST show a record row that has focus or was right-clicked the same way, and otherwise only what the page leaves out: the spec file, the raw header fields and the raw links both ways, plus a product's or domain's parent, children and problems, which its overview does not list.
