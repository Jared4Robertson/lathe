id: LA-EXP-UC11-R01
kind: requirement
title: The Model view is offered on domain, aggregate and entity pages only
parent: LA-EXP-UC11
group: Levels
---
A domain, aggregate or entity page MUST offer a Model view, a pane toggled like Wiki and Graph and sitting after them. Any other page MUST NOT offer it, and an address that names it there MUST show the page's other named views.
