---
id: LA-EXP-UC06-R07
kind: requirement
title: A technical flow's page shows its steps as a table per path, with each step's component and what it satisfies
parent: LA-EXP-UC06
---
A technical flow's page MUST show its steps as a flow's page does, a table per path in order, with the columns Step, What happens, Component and Satisfies, and each step's transaction, locks and statements folded under its row with its body.
