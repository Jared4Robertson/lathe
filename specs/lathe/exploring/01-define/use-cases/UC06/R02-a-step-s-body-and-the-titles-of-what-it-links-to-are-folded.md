id: LA-EXP-UC06-R02
kind: requirement
title: A step's body and linked titles fold under its row
parent: LA-EXP-UC06
---
Each step row MUST show one level: the step and the IDs it links to. Its body and the titles and kinds of what it uses and fulfills MUST be folded under the row's disclosure, with Expand all and Collapse all for the table.
