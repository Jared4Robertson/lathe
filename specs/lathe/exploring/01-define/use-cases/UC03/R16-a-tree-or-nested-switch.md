id: LA-EXP-UC03-R16
kind: requirement
title: A Tree or Nested switch shows parents beside their children or made of them
parent: LA-EXP-UC03
---
The graph's toolbar MUST offer a View switch of Tree and Nested, Tree by default, kept with the tab's graph settings (never in the address), with a command and its shortcut (`v`) in the command list. Tree draws the one map as an icicle, each parent beside its children (`LA-EXP-DR57`). Nested draws the same tree as boxes inside boxes: the product made of its domains, a domain of Define and Design, a stage of its folders, a folder of its records (`LA-EXP-DR62`), explored by the same camera (`LA-EXP-DR63`). Everything else the graph does MUST work the same in both: a click, Enter or a tap to focus, the hover spotlight with Details following it, the step keys, Tab and the arrow keys through the labeled boxes with a visible focus ring, `.` to frame the focus again, depth 1 to 3, Links, Filters (a hidden kind keeps its space), trace mode, the fit and zoom buttons, the minimap, the trail, Back and Forward with the camera's glide, the side bar's Details, Outline and History, the problem and coverage marks, and the same roles and accessible names.
