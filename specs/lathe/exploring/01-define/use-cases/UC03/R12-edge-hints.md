id: LA-EXP-UC03-R12
kind: requirement
title: Edge hints count the linked records past each edge
parent: LA-EXP-UC03
---
With the Links switch on, at each canvas edge a small chip MUST count the records the focus links to that lie past that edge ("3 ↑", "8 →"), update as the camera moves, and hide when nothing is past it. Clicking a chip MUST glide the camera, at its zoom, to bring the nearest of them into view, without changing the focus.
