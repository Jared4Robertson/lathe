id: LA-EXP-UC03-R04
kind: requirement
title: Node and line style show kind, relation, errors and gaps
parent: LA-EXP-UC03
---
Nodes MUST be colored and shaped by stage (Define, Design, structural), a domain drawn as a hexagon (see LA-EXP-DR61), lines MUST be styled by relation, and records with errors or coverage gaps MUST carry a visible mark. A legend MUST explain the styles.
