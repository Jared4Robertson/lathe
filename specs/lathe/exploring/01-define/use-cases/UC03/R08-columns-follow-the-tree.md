---
id: LA-EXP-UC03-R08
kind: requirement
title: The graph is one map in columns that follow the parent/child tree
parent: LA-EXP-UC03
---
The graph MUST be laid out left to right in columns by depth in the parent/child tree, one map for the product's tree (see LA-EXP-DR57): the product furthest left, then each level in its own column; the focus's ancestors to its left and its descendants to its right. Every record with the same parent, record or group, MUST sit in the same column whatever its kind (see LA-EXP-DR17). Parent/child lines MUST be drawn as the solid spine of the tree, passing through a group in its own column where the parent's children are grouped (see LA-EXP-UC04), and every other relation as a lighter, dashed cross-link. Links MUST NOT move anything: a linked record keeps its place in the map. In trace mode the graph MUST read left to right in its own columns: the requirement or rule, what fulfills it, the components, the tests, then the files.
