id: LA-EXP-UC03-R09
kind: requirement
title: Focusing moves the camera, not the map
parent: LA-EXP-UC03
group: Camera
---
When the person focuses a record or group, the map MUST hold still: no node moves or changes size, and the camera glides, in one smooth zoom and pan on the shared motion, to frame the new focus and the levels the depth shows below it (see LA-EXP-DR59). The first picture of a page appears at once. When the system asks for reduced motion, nothing MUST animate: the camera jumps to frame the focus (see LA-EXP-DR31).
