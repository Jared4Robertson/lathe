id: LA-EXP-UC03-R13
kind: requirement
title: Nodes hand off between focus and context without popping
parent: LA-EXP-UC03
---
On a re-center, a node going from focus to context MUST fade down and shrink as the camera moves, and one going from context to focus MUST fade up and grow, on the same clock as the move; nothing pops. Under reduced motion context nodes simply appear dimmed.
