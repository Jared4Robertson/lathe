id: LA-EXP-UC03-R15
kind: requirement
title: A minimap shows the whole map and where the view sits
parent: LA-EXP-UC03
---
A Minimap switch under Filters, off by default and kept per tab, MUST show a minimap in a corner: a true overview of the one map, its upper levels as boxes (as many as stay readable), the focus solid and the camera's view outlined as it moves, colored through the theme's tokens.
