title: Clicking a node re-centers the graph and changes the address
parent: LA-EXP-UC03
---
Clicking a record node MUST re-center the graph on that record and change the address to its graph page, so the browser's Back button walks back along the path. A breadcrumb trail MUST show the records and groups visited, named as LA-EXP-UC09-R07 says, and going back to one of them MUST cut the trail there.
