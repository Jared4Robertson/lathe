---
id: LA-EXP-UC03-R01
kind: requirement
title: Depth frames levels of the nav's tree below the focus
parent: LA-EXP-UC03
---
The graph view of `/<record-id>` (`?view=graph`, or its graph pane beside others) MUST show the one map of the nav's tree, stages and folders included (see LA-EXP-DR45, LA-EXP-DR57), and at depth N (1 by default, up to 3) MUST frame the record, its parent at the left edge and its descendants down to N levels below it (see LA-EXP-DR46), with no cross-links: at depth 1 its parent (or folder) and its children (or their folders). A Links switch beside Depth, off by default, MUST add the record's own links, one hop (see LA-EXP-DR49). `/graph` on its own MUST open the first product.
