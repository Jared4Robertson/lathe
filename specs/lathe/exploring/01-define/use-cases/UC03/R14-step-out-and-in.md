title: The keyboard steps out to the parent, in to a child and across to siblings
parent: LA-EXP-UC03
group: Controls
---
Alt+Up MUST re-center on the parent, Alt+Down on the first child, and Alt+Shift+Left and Alt+Shift+Right on the previous and next sibling in the nav's order (Alt+Left and Alt+Right stay the browser's Back and Forward). They MUST be keymap commands, shown in the shortcuts sheet, with buttons that carry tooltips and `aria-keyshortcuts`.
