The reader keeps several records, groups, domains or products open at once, as editors in VS Code, and flips between them without losing their place. A plain click opens a page in the preview tab, which the next plain click replaces; the reader keeps the tabs they want. Each tab keeps its own views, pane sizes, wiki scroll and graph settings, the address follows the active tab, and a reload brings them all back. The tabs sit at the left of the page bar above the panes; on a phone they are the Open pages sheet.
