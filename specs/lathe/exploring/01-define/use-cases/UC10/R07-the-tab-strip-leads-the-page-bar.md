id: LA-EXP-UC10-R07
kind: requirement
title: The tab strip leads the page bar, each tab with ID and title
parent: LA-EXP-UC10
---
The tab strip MUST sit at the left of the page bar above the panes, apart from the panes' own headers, which name their views. Each tab MUST show its record's ID in mono and its title, cut short with an ellipsis, with both in full in its tooltip; a group's tab MUST show its label ("Use cases (7)"). Tabs that do not fit MUST scroll sideways by wheel or trackpad, and the active tab MUST scroll into view; the page bar keeps its height. On a phone the tabs MUST fold into an "Open pages" button in the page bar that opens them as a sheet (`LA-PLT-UC07-R05`).
