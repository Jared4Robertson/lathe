kind: requirement
title: Each tab keeps its own views, pane sizes, wiki scroll and graph settings
parent: LA-EXP-UC10
---
Each tab MUST keep its own open views, pane sizes, wiki scroll position and graph settings (depth, trace, filters, open groups), and activating it MUST bring them back. A new tab MUST start from the active tab's views, sizes and settings, at the top of the page; a link that names views opens a new tab in those.
