The reader reads a product the way S2 lays out its specs. In the side nav, under the current space, Shared holds the patterns; each domain folds into Define (Domain model, Use cases) and Design (Interaction design, Technical specification, Test plan), then its open questions. The domain page and the group pages use the same folders and names as headings, so the page and the nav match.
