---
id: LA-EXP-UC08-R02
kind: requirement
title: Stages and folders fold one level at a time, with counts
parent: LA-EXP-UC08
---
Every domain, stage and folder MUST be a foldable row showing one level when opened; only the level under the space MUST show by default. Each stage and folder MUST show its count of records, and one with no records MUST NOT be listed.
