Jared works on one product at a time, as he works in one workspace at a time in Slack, Discord, Linear or Notion. A rail at the far left of the nav shows each product as a square icon; he chooses one and the nav, quick find and Checks follow it. He switches with Alt+1 to Alt+9 (⌥1 to ⌥9 on a Mac) or from the command palette, and comes back to the page he left.
