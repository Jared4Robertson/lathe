title: Alt and a number switch spaces
parent: LA-BRW-UC90
---
Alt+1 to Alt+9 (⌥1 to ⌥9 on a Mac) MUST switch to the first nine spaces in rail order, and the command palette MUST offer a "Switch to <product>" command for each, with its keys. Every other shortcut MUST keep working.
