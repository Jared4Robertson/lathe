The Reader keeps a side bar at the right of the page, as VS Code's secondary side bar, and picks one of its three views from a small switcher at its top. Details describes what the Reader is pointing at or has selected in the graph, the Model view or the wiki, without repeating what the page already shows. Outline lists what the main view holds, the wiki's sections or the graph's records by level or the diagram's boxes, as rows to jump to. History lists the commits that changed the record's spec file, the changes not committed yet first, and shows any one of them as a diff. The side bar starts closed in the Wiki view, which shows a record's facts itself, and open in the Graph and Model views; each view mode and each tab keeps the Reader's own choice.
