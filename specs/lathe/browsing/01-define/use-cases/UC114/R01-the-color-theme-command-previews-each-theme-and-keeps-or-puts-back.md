id: LA-BRW-UC114-R01
kind: requirement
title: The Color theme command previews each theme and keeps or puts back
parent: LA-BRW-UC114
---
The "Color theme" command (`chooseTheme`, g then t) MUST open a quick pick of every theme. Arrowing to a theme MUST show it on the whole app at once, without keeping it; Enter or a click MUST keep it, and Escape or a click outside MUST put back the theme shown before.
