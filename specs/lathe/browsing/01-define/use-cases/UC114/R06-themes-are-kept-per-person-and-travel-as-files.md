---
id: LA-BRW-UC114-R06
kind: requirement
title: Themes are kept per person and travel as files
parent: LA-BRW-UC114
---
His own themes, the chosen theme and the mode MUST be kept per person (in the browser's local storage, behind a store the desktop app can replace), and MUST survive a reload. Any theme MUST export as a small, versioned JSON file, and such a file MUST import as a new theme of his own.
