The reader opens the Model view on a domain and reads its domain model as an entity-relationship diagram: its aggregates and how their entities refer to each other. On an aggregate they read its entities with their fields and relationships, and on an entity the same diagram with that entity picked out. They click a box to go to it, and read the key under the diagram for the colors and line ends.
