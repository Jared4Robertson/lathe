Jared opens the Model view on a domain and reads its domain model as an entity-relationship diagram: its aggregates and how their entities refer to each other. On an aggregate he reads its entities with their fields and relationships, and on an entity the same diagram with that entity picked out. He clicks a box to go to it, and reads the key under the diagram for the colors and line ends.
