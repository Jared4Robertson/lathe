title: Every page passes the audit
parent: LA-BRW-UC120

The product, domain, record and group pages, the graph and the Model view, Checks, Format and Appearance, quick find, the command palette, the shortcuts sheet, a tooltip, the side bar and the phone's sheets MUST pass axe-core's WCAG 2.2 AA rules, at 1440 px and at 375 px, in the Lathe theme and the High contrast theme, each in light and in dark.
