id: LA-BRW-UC120-R04
kind: requirement
title: What changes out of sight is announced
parent: LA-BRW-UC120
---
When a command changes the page somewhere other than where focus is (a view opened or closed, the inspector shown or hidden, a theme chosen, every row folded), the app's one announcer (a polite live region) MUST say so in a short sentence, such as "Graph view", "Inspector hidden" or "Color theme: Midnight".
