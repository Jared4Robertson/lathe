kind: requirement
title: Kinds, problem codes and required fields link to their format pages
parent: LA-BRW-UC115
---
The side bar's Kind row MUST link to the kind's page, on a record page and on a group page. Every problem's code on the Checks page, the domain page and the record page MUST link to its check's page. A record's required field labels MUST link to that field on its kind's page.
