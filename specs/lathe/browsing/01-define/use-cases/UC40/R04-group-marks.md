---
id: LA-BRW-UC40-R04
kind: requirement
title: A group carries a mark when anything inside it has problems or gaps
parent: LA-BRW-UC40
---
A group MUST carry the error, warning and coverage-gap marks that records carry whenever any member, or any record beneath a member, has them, so a folded group still shows where the trouble is. The legend MUST explain groups and their marks.
