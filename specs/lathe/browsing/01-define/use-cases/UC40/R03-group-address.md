---
id: LA-BRW-UC40-R03
kind: requirement
title: A group has its own address and can be the centre
parent: LA-BRW-UC40
---
A group MUST be the focus at `/graph/<parent-id>/<kind>`, for example `/graph/TL-LEND/use_case`, showing the parent to the left and all the group's members to the right, with a side panel that names the parent and lists every member. The focused record's side panel MUST link to its groups. The breadcrumb trail and the browser's Back button MUST work for groups as for records. An address for a group that does not exist MUST say so.
