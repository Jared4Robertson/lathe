---
id: LA-BRW-UC80-R01
kind: requirement
title: "A flow's page shows its steps in order as a table"
parent: LA-BRW-UC80
---
A flow's record page MUST show its steps, when it has any, as a table in step order with the columns Step (order and ID), What happens (the title), Uses and Fulfills, each linked ID going to its record.
