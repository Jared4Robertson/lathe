id: LA-BRW-UC80-R04
kind: requirement
title: In the graph a flow step sits under its flow
parent: LA-BRW-UC80
---
In the graph a flow step MUST sit one column to the right of its flow, joined to it by the parent line, and, as for other children, MUST reach them through a folded group only when the flow also has children of another kind.
