---
id: LA-BRW-UC50-R03
kind: requirement
title: Split has a draggable divider, and either pane can go full screen
parent: LA-BRW-UC50
---
Split MUST show the wiki and the graph side by side with a divider that can be dragged, moved with the arrow keys (Home and End for the limits) and double-clicked back to the middle; its position MUST be remembered. Each pane MUST have a full screen button; Escape or the same button MUST return to the view it came from, with focus back on the button.
