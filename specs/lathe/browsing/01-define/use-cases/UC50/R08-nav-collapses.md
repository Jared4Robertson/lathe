id: LA-BRW-UC50-R08
kind: requirement
title: The side navigation collapses to a rail, and is a drawer on a narrow screen
parent: LA-BRW-UC50
---
A toggle MUST collapse the nav to a narrow rail of icons (the logo, Quick find, Diagrams, Specs, Checks, Record format), each with a tooltip and an accessible name, and expand it again; the state MUST be remembered. Under about 900 px wide the nav MUST become a drawer opened from a menu button, with focus moved into it, and closed by Escape, a click outside or choosing a page, with focus returned to the menu button.
