id: LA-BRW-UC50-R07
kind: requirement
title: Every list that can grow stays at ten rows, in every view
parent: LA-BRW-UC50
---
Every list that can grow, including the inspector's lists (children, links, members, problems, what the graph connects, the files a trace reaches) and a group page's members, MUST use the long list: ten rows, then a scroll inside with a search box and a count.
