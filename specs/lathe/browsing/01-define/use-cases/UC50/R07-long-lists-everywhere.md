title: Every list that can grow stays at ten rows, in every view
parent: LA-BRW-UC50
---
Every list that can grow, including the side bar's lists (raw links, a product's or domain's children, problems, what the graph connects, the files a trace reaches, the outline's rows) and a group page's members, MUST use the long list: ten rows, then a scroll inside with a search box and a count.
