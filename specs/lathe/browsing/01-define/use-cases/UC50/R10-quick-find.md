---
id: LA-BRW-UC50-R10
kind: requirement
title: Quick find jumps to anything by ID or title
parent: LA-BRW-UC50
---
A search field at the top of the nav MUST find any record, domain, product or diagram by ID or title, best matches first (exact ID, ID prefix, title), and MUST let the person move through the results with the arrow keys and open one with Enter.
