---
id: LA-BRW-UC50-R11
kind: requirement
title: The inspector is a third column that folds away
parent: LA-BRW-UC50
---
A record, group, product or domain page MUST have three columns: the nav, the main view and the inspector. The inspector MUST show the focused record's kind, fields, spec file, parent, children, links out and in, problems and coverage gaps (for a group: its parent, kind, members and their problems), and, while the graph shows, what the graph connects to it and the legend. One inspector MUST serve the page in every view. It MUST fold away with a toggle on its edge and a shortcut, remember that, give its space to the main view, and on a narrow screen open as a bottom sheet that takes focus, keeps it inside, and closes with Escape (`LA-BRW-UC116-R04`).
