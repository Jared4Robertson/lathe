---
id: LA-BRW-UC50-R11
kind: requirement
title: The inspector is a side bar that folds away
parent: LA-BRW-UC50
---
A record, group, product or domain page MUST have three columns: the nav, the main view and the inspector, a side bar with the views Details, Outline and History (`LA-BRW-UC119`). One side bar MUST serve the page in every view, and show nothing the main view already shows beside it (`LA-BRW-DR340`). It MUST fold away with a toggle in the page bar and a shortcut, remember that per view mode and per tab (`LA-BRW-UC119-R02`), give its space to the main view, and on a narrow screen open as a bottom sheet that takes focus, keeps it inside, and closes with Escape (`LA-BRW-UC116-R04`).
