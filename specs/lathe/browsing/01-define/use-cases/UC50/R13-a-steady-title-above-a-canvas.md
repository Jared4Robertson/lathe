id: LA-BRW-UC50-R13
kind: requirement
title: The title above a graph or a model keeps one height
parent: LA-BRW-UC50
---
When the page's title sits at the top of a graph or a model pane, it MUST keep one fixed height whatever the record, each line cut with an ellipsis and the whole title in its tooltip, so the canvas below never moves when the focus changes. In the wiki pane the title MAY wrap.
