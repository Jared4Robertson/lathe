title: Both panes follow one focus, and Back and Forward move both
parent: LA-BRW-UC50
---
Clicking a record in the graph MUST change the address, so the wiki pane and the inspector follow; following a link in the wiki MUST re-center the graph. Back and Forward MUST move both panes. The graph's depth, filters, open groups and trace mode MUST survive moving between pages and switching views.
