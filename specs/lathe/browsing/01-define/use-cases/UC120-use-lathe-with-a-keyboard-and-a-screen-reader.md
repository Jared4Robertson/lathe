The Reader works without a mouse, or listens to the page through a screen reader, or both. They move through the nav, the page bar, the panes and the side bar with Tab and the arrow keys; open a record from the nav or quick find; switch views; re-center the graph; fold rows; show and hide the side bar; and pick a theme. Every control says what it is and what it does in words that match what is drawn, every part that scrolls can be reached, nothing holds focus, focus lands where the Reader expects after each move and comes back to where it was when a dialog or a sheet closes, and what changes out of sight is announced. The page reads as clearly in the High contrast theme, in light and dark, on a desk and on a phone. The findings behind the design are in `notes/a11y-audit.md`.
