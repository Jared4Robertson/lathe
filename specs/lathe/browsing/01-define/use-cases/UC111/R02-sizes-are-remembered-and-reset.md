id: LA-BRW-UC111-R02
kind: requirement
title: Sizes are remembered, and double-click resets one
parent: LA-BRW-UC111
---
Each panel's size MUST be remembered across visits (each pane's as a fraction of the panes' width, so it holds whichever panes are open), a remembered size outside its limits MUST fall back to the default, and double-clicking a gap MUST reset its size to the default (navigation 304 px, side bar 340 px), or for a gap between two panes share their space evenly.
