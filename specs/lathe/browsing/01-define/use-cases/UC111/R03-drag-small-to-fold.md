id: LA-BRW-UC111-R03
kind: requirement
title: Dragging the navigation or the inspector small folds it
parent: LA-BRW-UC111
---
Dragging the navigation below 180 px MUST fold it to its rail, and dragging the inspector below 160 px MUST hide it; dragging the rail's gap out again MUST unfold the navigation. On a narrow screen there MUST be no gaps to drag: the navigation and the inspector are drawers.
