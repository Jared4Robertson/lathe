---
id: LA-BRW-UC118-R05
kind: requirement
title: Every link is shown by its label
parent: LA-BRW-UC118
---
The record facts beside the body, the inspector's Links out and Links in, the graph's edge labels, legend and connections, and the Model view's line titles MUST name links by their relation's `forward_label` (from the source) or `reverse_label` (from the target), never by the relation's code.
