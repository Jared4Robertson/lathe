id: LA-BRW-UC112-R01
kind: requirement
title: A plain open uses the preview tab, and a page open in a tab activates it
parent: LA-BRW-UC112
---
Opening a page by a plain click (a record link, a graph node or box, the nav, quick find, or Back and Forward) MUST open it in the preview tab, whose title is italic, replacing that tab's page in place; with no preview tab, a new one MUST open after the active tab. A page already open in a tab MUST activate that tab instead, as it was. Cmd- or Ctrl-click and middle-click on a link MUST still open a new browser tab.
