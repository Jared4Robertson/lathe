---
id: LA-BRW-UC112-R07
kind: requirement
title: The tab strip leads the leftmost pane's bar, each tab with ID and title
parent: LA-BRW-UC112
---
The tab strip MUST sit at the left of the leftmost pane's title bar, in place of that pane's view name; the other panes' bars keep their view names. Each tab MUST show its record's ID in mono and its title, cut short with an ellipsis, with both in full in its tooltip; a group's tab MUST show its label ("Use cases (7)"). Tabs that do not fit MUST scroll sideways by wheel or trackpad, and the active tab MUST scroll into view.
