---
id: LA-BRW-UC112-R03
kind: requirement
title: A tab closes by its button, a middle-click or Alt+Shift+W
parent: LA-BRW-UC112
---
Each tab MUST have a close button, shown on hover, on focus and on the active tab. Middle-clicking a tab MUST close it, and Alt+Shift+W (`closeTab`) MUST close the active tab; never Ctrl+W, which toggles the Wiki view on a Mac and closes the browser tab elsewhere. Closing the active tab MUST activate its neighbor to the right, or else to the left, and move the address there.
