id: LA-BRW-UC112-R02
kind: requirement
title: A tab is kept by double-click, the Keep open command or switching its views
parent: LA-BRW-UC112
---
The preview tab MUST become a lasting tab, which the next plain open no longer replaces, when Jared double-clicks it, runs the "Keep the tab open" command (`keepTab`: Ctrl+K then Enter on a Mac, as in VS Code, and Alt+K then Enter elsewhere, where Ctrl+K is quick open), or opens or closes one of its views.
