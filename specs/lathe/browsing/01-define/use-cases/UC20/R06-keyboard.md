kind: requirement
title: The graph works from the keyboard
parent: LA-BRW-UC20
---
Arrow keys or Tab MUST move between nodes, Enter MUST re-centre on the focused record (or open or close the focused group, see LA-BRW-UC40-R02), and Escape MUST clear the filters and trace mode. Keyboard focus MUST always be visible.
