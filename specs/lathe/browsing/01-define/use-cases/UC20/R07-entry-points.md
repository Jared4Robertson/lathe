id: LA-BRW-UC20-R07
kind: requirement
title: Every page opens the graph in its Graph view, not from the nav
parent: LA-BRW-UC20
---
Every record, group, product and domain page MUST open its graph in its own Graph view (the Graph view toggle in the page bar, Ctrl+G or Alt+G, Ctrl+2 and the command palette), and the older `/graph` addresses MUST keep leading there. The side navigation MUST NOT have a Graph item, open or as a rail: the graph is a view of a page, not a place of its own.
