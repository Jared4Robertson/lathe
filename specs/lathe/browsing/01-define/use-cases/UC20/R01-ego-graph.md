---
id: LA-BRW-UC20-R01
kind: requirement
title: The graph shows a record and its neighbours to depth 1 or 2
parent: LA-BRW-UC20
---
The graph view at `/graph/<record-id>` MUST show the record and every record within the chosen depth, following outgoing links, incoming links and the parent/child relation, with every link between the records shown. Depth MUST be 1 by default and switchable to 2. `/graph` on its own MUST open the first product.
