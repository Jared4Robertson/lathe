id: LA-BRW-UC20-R02

title: Clicking a node re-centres the graph and changes the address
parent: LA-BRW-UC20
---
Clicking a record node MUST re-centre the graph on that record and change the address to its graph page, so the browser's Back button walks back along the path. A breadcrumb trail MUST show the records and groups visited, and going back to one of them MUST cut the trail there.
