id: LA-BRW-UC20-R11
kind: requirement
title: The context ring stays light, with chips for the rest
parent: LA-BRW-UC20
---
A parent with more than 6 context children MUST show the first 6, in the nav's order, and a chip such as "+12 more use cases"; clicking the chip MUST re-center on that parent's folder for those children. Chips are context: faded, out of the keyboard order, and clickable. The ring MUST hold at most about 40 context nodes, chips included (see LA-BRW-DR305).
