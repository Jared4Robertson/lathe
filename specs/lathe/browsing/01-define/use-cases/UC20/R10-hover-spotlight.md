id: LA-BRW-UC20-R10
kind: requirement
title: Hovering a node spotlights it and what it joins
parent: LA-BRW-UC20
---
Hovering or keyboard-focusing a focus node MUST brighten it, its edges and the nodes it joins directly, focus or context, and dim the rest a step; leaving MUST restore the picture. It MUST change with the motion tokens, and at once under reduced motion.
