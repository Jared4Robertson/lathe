id: LA-BRW-UC20-R12
kind: requirement
title: Edge hints count the faded nodes past each edge
parent: LA-BRW-UC20
---
At each canvas edge a small chip MUST count the context nodes past that edge ("3 ↑", "8 →"), update as the camera moves, and hide when nothing is past it. Clicking a chip MUST pan the camera that way by about half the canvas, with the shared motion, without changing the focus.
