The reader opens a domain and sees which patterns its use cases follow, and for each pattern rule whether the domain designs, builds and proves it or marks it not applicable, as S2's "Patterns we follow" tables do. They open a pattern to see its settings, its rules and who follows it. Reading any record, they follow the IDs its prose cites with one click, and see at a glance which cited IDs no longer exist.
