The Reader opens pages, follows links, re-centers the graph, opens and closes panes, folds rows and resizes panels, and the specs reload under them as they change. Whatever changes, what the Reader is looking at stays where it was: the frame of the page holds still, new content takes the room kept for it, and anything that does move glides there on the shared motion. A header that grew with its title, a key that resized the diagram above it and a picture drawn once in the wrong place and then moved are the kind of jitter this rules out.
