id: LA-BRW-UC113-R06
kind: requirement
title: The ERD shows the next ring out, faded, and frames what is in focus
parent: LA-BRW-UC113
---
On an aggregate's ERD, the entities that its referenced entities refer to MUST appear one ring out, faded and out of focus, with their lines faded. On a domain's ERD, the aggregates of other domains MUST be faded context too. The camera MUST frame the boxes in focus only, and edge hints MUST count the faded boxes past each edge (see LA-BRW-DR302, LA-BRW-DR303).
