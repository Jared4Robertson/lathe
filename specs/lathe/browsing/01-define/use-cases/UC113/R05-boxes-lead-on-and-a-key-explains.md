title: Boxes lead to their pages, and a key explains the diagram
parent: LA-BRW-UC113
---
Clicking a box, or pressing Enter on it, MUST go to its page, keeping the open views, and a box MUST show a hover state; the highlighted entity on its own page leads nowhere. A key under the diagram MUST explain the colors, with 🟦 for the current domain and ⬜ for another, and the line ends.
