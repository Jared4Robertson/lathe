id: LA-BRW-UC110-R07
kind: requirement
title: The graph trail names what was visited
parent: LA-BRW-UC110
---
The graph's trail of visited records MUST show a record as its title with its ID in parentheses, such as "Browsing (LA-BRW)", and a group by its name alone, without a count, such as "Use cases", naming its parent in its tooltip and accessible name. A long name MUST be cut short with an ellipsis, with the full text in its tooltip, and the current item MUST stay bold.
