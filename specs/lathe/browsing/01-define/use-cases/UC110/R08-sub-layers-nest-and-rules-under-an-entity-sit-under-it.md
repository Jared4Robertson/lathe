---
id: LA-BRW-UC110-R08
kind: requirement
title: Sub-layers nest, and rules under an entity sit under it
parent: LA-BRW-UC110
---
A layer inside another layer MUST show as a group within that layer's group, in its order, in the nav and on the domain page alike, and a component's branch MUST open through each layer above it. A domain rule under an entity MUST be listed under that entity wherever the entity's children are, on the domain and aggregate pages, and its nav branch MUST open to the aggregate holding it.
