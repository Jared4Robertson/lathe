---
id: LA-BRW-UC30-R01
kind: requirement
title: Coverage shows one group per obligation kind
parent: LA-BRW-UC30
---
Wherever coverage appears (the product page's domain cards and the domain page), it MUST show one group of bars per obligation kind the engine reports, labelled with the kind's label, each with designed, built, proved and planned counts.

While the engine reports no counts per obligation kind, coverage MUST fall back to requirements and domain rules with designed, built and proved, and MUST NOT show a planned count.
