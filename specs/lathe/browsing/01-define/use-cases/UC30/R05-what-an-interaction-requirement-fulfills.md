---
id: LA-BRW-UC30-R05
kind: requirement
title: The record page shows what an interaction requirement fulfills
parent: LA-BRW-UC30
---
For an interaction requirement, the record page MUST show the view, endpoint or flow it sits at and each requirement or domain rule it fulfills, with its kind. When it fulfills nothing, the page MUST say so and how to link it.
