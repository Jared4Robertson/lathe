id: LA-BRW-UC60-R08
kind: requirement
title: The graph shows pattern rules under their pattern and follows as a cross-link
parent: LA-BRW-UC60
---
In the graph a pattern MUST sit in the use cases' tier with its rules one tier to its right under it, and a use case's `follows` link MUST be drawn as a cross-link within that tier, in its own line style named in the legend, without shaping the tiers. Trace mode MUST be offered for a pattern rule, showing what satisfies and proves it in every domain.
