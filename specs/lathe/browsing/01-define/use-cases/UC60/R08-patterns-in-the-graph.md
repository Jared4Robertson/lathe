id: LA-BRW-UC60-R08
kind: requirement
title: The graph shows pattern rules under their pattern and follows as a cross-link
parent: LA-BRW-UC60
---
In the graph a pattern's rules MUST sit one column to its right, under it, and a use case's `follows` link MUST be drawn as a cross-link, in its own line style named in the legend; a use case reached from the pattern by that link sits one column after it. Trace mode MUST be offered for a pattern rule, showing what satisfies and proves it in every domain.
