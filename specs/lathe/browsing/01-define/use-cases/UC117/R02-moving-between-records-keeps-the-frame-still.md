---
id: LA-BRW-UC117-R02
kind: requirement
title: Moving between records keeps the frame still
parent: LA-BRW-UC117
---
Moving to another record through the nav, a link, a graph click, Back or quick find MUST keep the nav, the pane bars and the side bar where they were, and MUST draw the new page at its place from its first frame: a new page at the top, and a page reached by Back or Forward where it was scrolled. The nav's rows, the View toggles and the tabs' close buttons MUST NOT move: a View toggle the page does not offer keeps its slot, dimmed, and every tab has one width.
