kind: requirement
title: The first load shifts nothing
parent: LA-BRW-UC117
---
When a product, domain or record page first loads, nothing already drawn MAY move: content that arrives late (the specs, the diagrams, the fonts) MUST take room kept for it or appear without pushing what is drawn. The nav draws its lists once the specs and the diagrams have both arrived; the fonts are the app's own, preloaded, and a font that is not there in time is not swapped in later.
