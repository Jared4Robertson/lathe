Jared picks how Lathe looks, as he does in VS Code: one of a few built-in themes (Lathe, High contrast, Workshop, Midnight and Graphite), and Light, Dark or the system's choice. He arrows through the themes and sees each one at once. When none suits him, he duplicates one and changes its colors, fonts, text size, density, corners, gaps and motion, with a warning wherever text gets hard to read, and shares the result as a file.
