Jared opens any record, group, domain or product at its one address and chooses to read it (Wiki), see it among its neighbors (Graph), or both side by side (Split). Following a link in either pane moves both, and the inspector beside them always shows the record's facts. He moves around with a side navigation that folds away, finds anything by ID or title, and reads each page one level deep, opening only what he wants.
