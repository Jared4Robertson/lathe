The reader opens any record, group, domain or product at its one address and chooses how to read it, opening and closing views as panes side by side: as a page (Wiki), among its neighbors (Graph), and on a domain, aggregate or entity as an ERD (Model). Following a link in any pane moves them all, and the side bar beside them always shows the record's facts. They move around with a side navigation that folds away, find anything by ID or title, and read each page one level deep, opening only what they want.
