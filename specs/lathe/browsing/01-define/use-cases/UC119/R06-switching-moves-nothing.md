id: LA-BRW-UC119-R06
kind: requirement
title: Switching the side bar's view or what it describes moves nothing outside it
parent: LA-BRW-UC119
---
Switching between Details, Outline and History, and Details following the pointer or the selection, MUST change only the side bar's content: the nav, the panes, their bars and titles, the canvas, the side bar's own box and its switcher MUST hold still (`LA-PAT-APP-UX-R08`). A hover MUST redraw only the side bar, never the canvas.
