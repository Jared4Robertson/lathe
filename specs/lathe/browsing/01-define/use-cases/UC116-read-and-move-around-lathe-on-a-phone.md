Away from a desk, the reader opens Lathe on a phone to read, not to arrange panels: they follow a record's links, check a domain's coverage, look at a graph or an ERD, and find a record by ID or title. One view fills the screen at a time; the nav, the inspector, the open pages and quick find come up as sheets and go away again; everything answers a thumb, and nothing needs a hover. On a tablet in landscape they keep the workbench, with targets sized for a finger. The decisions behind the design are in `notes/mobile-design.md`.
