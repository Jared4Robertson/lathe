Away from his desk, Jared opens Lathe on his phone to read, not to arrange panels: he follows a record's links, checks a domain's coverage, looks at a graph or an ERD, and finds a record by ID or title. One view fills the screen at a time; the nav, the inspector, his open pages and quick find come up as sheets and go away again; everything answers a thumb, and nothing needs a hover. On a tablet in landscape he keeps the workbench, with targets sized for a finger. The decisions behind the design are in `notes/mobile-design.md`.
