---
id: LA-BRW-UC116-R08
kind: requirement
title: Pages are readable on a phone
parent: LA-BRW-UC116
---
On a phone body text MUST be at least 16 px, and every text box MUST be 16 px so the phone does not zoom in on focus. Long IDs MUST wrap rather than widen the page; wide tables and code blocks MUST scroll sideways inside their own box; long lists MUST keep their ten rows with a search (`LA-BRW-DR71`).
