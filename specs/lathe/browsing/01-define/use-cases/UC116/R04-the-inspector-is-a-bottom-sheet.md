---
id: LA-BRW-UC116-R04
kind: requirement
title: The inspector is a bottom sheet at half or full height
parent: LA-BRW-UC116
---
On a phone the inspector MUST open from the page bar as a bottom sheet with a grab handle, at half the screen's height. Dragging the handle up or tapping it MUST take it to full height; dragging it down MUST take it back to half, or close it from half; a flick MUST count as a long drag. Escape, a tap on the dimmed page above it or its close button MUST close it. It MUST take focus as it opens, keep Tab inside it, and give focus back to its toggle when it closes.
