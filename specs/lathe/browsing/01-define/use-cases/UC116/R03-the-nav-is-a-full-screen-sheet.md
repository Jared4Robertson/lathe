---
id: LA-BRW-UC116-R03
kind: requirement
title: The nav is a full-screen sheet with the same rows, sized for a thumb
parent: LA-BRW-UC116
---
On a phone the nav MUST open from the top bar's menu button as a full-screen sheet that slides in from the left, keeps the space rail, takes focus to its quick find, keeps Tab inside it, and closes by Escape, its close button or choosing a page, giving focus back to the menu button. Its rows MUST be the wide screen's rows (`LA-BRW-DR220`), at least 44 px tall on a touch screen, with the fold chevrons as large targets.
