title: The browser chrome follows the theme, and sheets move only when motion is welcome
parent: LA-BRW-UC116
---
The page MUST carry a `theme-color` meta that follows the theme's sheet color, in light and dark and for any theme chosen. Sheets MUST slide in with the shared motion tokens and MUST appear at once when the system asks for reduced motion (`LA-PAT-APP-CODE-R12`).
