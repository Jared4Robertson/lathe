The reader sizes the navigation, the side bar and the open panes (Wiki, Graph, Model) to suit what they are reading, as in VS Code: they drag the gap between two panels, or focus it and use the keyboard. Lathe remembers the sizes, and dragging the navigation or the side bar small enough folds it away.
