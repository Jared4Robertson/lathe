---
id: LA-BRW-DR340
kind: domain_rule
title: The side bar never repeats what the page shows
parent: LA-BRW-A11
---
What the side bar shows MUST earn its place: no list or fact the main view already shows beside it (a record's children, its problems, its links grouped by label in the wiki's Traceability). It describes what the main view does not: the node under the pointer or selected, the file behind the page, the page's outline, and the file's history.
