---
id: LA-BRW-DR280
kind: domain_rule
title: A product and a domain have one page, at their ID
parent: LA-BRW-A1
---
A product's and a domain's only page MUST be their overview, at their bare ID (`/LA`, `/LA-BRW`); there MUST NOT be a second, generic record page for them. That one page MUST lose nothing the generic page showed: its body in the wiki, and in the side bar its kind, fields, spec file, parent, children, links out and in, and problems.
