---
id: LA-BRW-DR101
kind: domain_rule
title: "A space's icon is its own or its monogram"
parent: LA-BRW-A1
---
A product's icon MUST be its `icon` field when that is one emoji or up to two characters, and otherwise its ID as a monogram. Its colour MUST be its `color` field when that is a hex colour or a colour name, and otherwise one derived from its ID, the same on every visit.
