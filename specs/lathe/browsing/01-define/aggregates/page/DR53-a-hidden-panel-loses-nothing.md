---
id: LA-BRW-DR53
kind: domain_rule
title: Hiding a panel gives its space away and loses nothing
parent: LA-BRW-A1
---
Hiding the nav or the inspector MUST give its space to the main view and MUST keep a control on the same edge that brings it back. The inspector MUST show the same record whichever view is open, so hiding a pane never hides the record's facts for good.
