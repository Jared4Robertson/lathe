---
id: LA-BRW-DR80
kind: domain_rule
title: A count the engine does not report is unknown, never zero
parent: LA-BRW-A1
---
A page MUST show a count the engine does not report, such as `tests` or `planned_any` from an older engine, as not yet measured or not at all, never as zero. A total MUST be unknown when any count in it is.
