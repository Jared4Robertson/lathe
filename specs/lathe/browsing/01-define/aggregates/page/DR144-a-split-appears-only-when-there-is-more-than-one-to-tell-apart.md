---
id: LA-BRW-DR144
kind: domain_rule
title: A split appears only when there is more than one to tell apart
parent: LA-BRW-A1
---
A list MUST be split by kind, or by a computed group such as a test group, only when it holds more than one kind or group to tell apart; with one, its records MUST be listed directly, with no level, heading, row, tag or graph folder for the one kind or group. Groups that are records (layers, capabilities, use cases, patterns) and the fixed stages and folders are not such splits and stay.
