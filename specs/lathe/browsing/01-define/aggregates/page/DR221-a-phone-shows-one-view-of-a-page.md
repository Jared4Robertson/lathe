---
id: LA-BRW-DR221
kind: domain_rule
title: A phone shows one view of a page at a time
parent: LA-BRW-A1
---
On a screen too narrow for panes side by side, a page MUST show exactly one of its views, the first of those its address names, and the address MUST name the view shown. The views a page has open on a wide screen are not lost by reading it on a phone.
