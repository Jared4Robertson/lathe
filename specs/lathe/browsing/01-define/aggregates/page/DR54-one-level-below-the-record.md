---
id: LA-BRW-DR54
kind: domain_rule
title: A wiki page shows one level below its record by default
parent: LA-BRW-A1
---
A wiki page (product, domain, record or group) and the inspector MUST show by default only the records one level below the page's own record. Anything deeper MUST be folded under its row and open only when the person opens it, opens everything, or searches for something inside it.
