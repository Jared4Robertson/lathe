---
id: LA-BRW-DR245
kind: domain_rule
title: Traceability groups follow the meta-model's roles
parent: LA-BRW-A1
---
Which links go in which Traceability group, and in what order, MUST follow only the meta-model: a relation's `design` or `proof` role, a test's `supports` links, and whether the record's kind is an obligation. A group is titled by its links' label, holds each record once, and is left out when empty.
