---
id: LA-BRW-DR323
kind: domain_rule
title: A target is at least 24 px, or has room around it
parent: LA-BRW-A13
---
On any pointer, a link or control that is not inside a sentence MUST be at least 24 by 24 px, or stand far enough from its neighbors that a 24 px circle on it touches no other target (WCAG 2.2, 2.5.8). Record links listed in a table cell or the side bar are drawn one line tall at 24 px. On a coarse pointer the larger 44 px of `LA-PAT-APP-UX-R07` applies.
