---
id: LA-BRW-DR153
kind: domain_rule
title: The address names the active tab
parent: LA-BRW-A8
---
The address MUST name the active tab's page and, once its views are named, its views: activating, cycling or closing a tab moves the address, and a link to a page open in another tab shows that tab as it was and rewrites the address to its views. Back to a page whose tab was closed reopens it as the preview tab.
