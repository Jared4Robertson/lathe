---
id: LA-BRW-DR307
kind: domain_rule
title: Domain events are drawn in the spark colors
parent: LA-BRW-A12
---
Wherever a domain event is drawn as a node or box (the ERD, the graph), it MUST be a rounded pill in the `--spark` and `--spark-bg` tokens, and its `raised_by` and `reacts_to` lines MUST be drawn in `--spark`, the `reacts_to` lines dashed, so events read the same in every view.
