---
id: LA-BRW-DR301
kind: domain_rule
title: "Depth counts levels of the tree each way"
parent: LA-BRW-A12
---
Depth N MUST show, in focus, the focus's ancestors up to N levels and its descendants down to N levels, every stage, folder and group counting as a level, as in a file explorer. Depth MUST NOT add siblings: they come only from a parent's folder when the person opens it. A group the person folds hides what is beneath it, and one they open shows its children. Depth stays 1 to 3, and a group at the center counts levels the same way.
