---
id: LA-BRW-DR100
kind: domain_rule
title: "The current space is the page's product"
parent: LA-BRW-A2
---
The current space MUST be the product of the page on screen when it belongs to one (its product, domain, record or group page); on a page above every product (Checks, the format, Diagrams) it MUST stay the space last chosen, or the first product.
