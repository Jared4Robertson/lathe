id: LA-BRW-DR244
kind: domain_rule
title: A gap callout repeats coverage's judgment
parent: LA-BRW-A6
---
A Traceability gap callout MUST come from coverage's own gaps for the domain that owes the obligation (and from the `component-file-missing` check for built), never from a count of links of its own, so it never disagrees with the coverage marks, the domain's Coverage line or the checks. A pattern rule is judged in each domain that owes it, from that domain's components and tests only.
