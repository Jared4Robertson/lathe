---
id: LA-BRW-DR160
kind: domain_rule
title: A list of one kind is titled by that kind
parent: LA-BRW-A3
---
A list of a record's children or a group's members MUST be titled by its kind's plural label, through `plural()`, when it holds one kind ("Domain rules", "UC requirements", "Components"), and MUST be titled "Children" or "Members" only when it holds more than one kind to tell apart. Its count stays beside the title, and its accessible name and search box follow the title ("Domain rules of LA-BRW-A1", "Filter domain rules").
