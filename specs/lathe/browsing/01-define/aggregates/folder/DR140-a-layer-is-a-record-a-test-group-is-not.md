---
id: LA-BRW-DR140
kind: domain_rule
title: A layer is a record, a test group is not
parent: LA-BRW-A3
---
A group that is a record (a layer, a capability, a use case) MUST link to its page wherever it heads its members; a group computed by the app (a test group) MUST NOT pose as a record: its nav row only folds and its heading is not a link.
