id: LA-BRW-DR147
kind: domain_rule
title: A line stays on its two nodes while the graph moves
parent: LA-BRW-A4
---
A line in the graph MUST join the edges of its two node boxes in every frame, including while the nodes move to a new picture: a line that persists moves from where it was drawn to where it goes on the same clock and easing as its nodes, and is never drawn apart from the boxes it joins.
