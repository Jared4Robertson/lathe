---
id: LA-BRW-DR148
kind: domain_rule
title: Motion carries no meaning and is turned off under reduced motion
parent: LA-BRW-A4
---
Motion in the graph MUST only help the eye follow a change: the picture after a move MUST be the same with or without it, and when the system asks for reduced motion the graph MUST jump to the new picture with no tween or fade.
