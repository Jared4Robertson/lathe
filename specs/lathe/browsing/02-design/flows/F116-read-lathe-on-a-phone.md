How Jared reads a domain on his phone between meetings. He taps the search button in the top bar, types a use case's ID and taps the result. The wiki fills the screen under the title; he reads its requirements, then taps Graph in the page bar. He pinches out, taps a component to see its name, and taps Open. He pulls up the inspector to check its coverage gaps, drags it to full height, then swipes it away. The link at the top takes him up to the domain; the Pages button brings back the use case he started from.
