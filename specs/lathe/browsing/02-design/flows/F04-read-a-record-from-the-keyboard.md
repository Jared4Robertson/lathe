1. The reader opens quick find from the keyboard, types part of an ID or title, and opens the record at its one address.
2. They open and close the Wiki, Graph and Model panes with their shortcuts; following a link in any pane moves them all, and the side bar shows the record's facts.
3. On a domain or an aggregate they open the Model view and read its ERD, then click a box to go to that aggregate or entity.
4. They keep the record open in a tab with Ctrl+K then Enter, open another in the preview tab, flip between them with Ctrl+Tab, each as they left it, and close one with Alt+Shift+W.
5. They fold the side navigation away, go back with the usual key, and open the shortcut sheet or the command palette when they need a key they do not know.
6. Throughout, Tab reaches every control and lets go of it, focus stays where they were after each move and comes back to the opener when a dialog or a sheet closes, and the announcer tells a screen reader what changed.
