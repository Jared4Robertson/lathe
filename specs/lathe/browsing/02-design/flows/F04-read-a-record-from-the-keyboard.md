1. Jared opens quick find from the keyboard, types part of an ID or title, and opens the record at its one address.
2. He opens and closes the Wiki, Graph and Model panes with their shortcuts; following a link in any pane moves them all, and the inspector shows the record's facts.
3. On a domain or an aggregate he opens the Model view and reads its ERD, then clicks a box to go to that aggregate or entity.
4. He keeps the record open in a tab with Ctrl+K then Enter, opens another in the preview tab, flips between them with Ctrl+Tab, each as he left it, and closes one with Alt+Shift+W.
5. He folds the side navigation away, goes back with the usual key, and opens the shortcut sheet or the command palette when he needs a key he does not know.
