1. Jared opens quick find from the keyboard, types part of an ID or title, and opens the record at its one address.
2. He switches between the Wiki, Graph and Split views with their shortcuts; following a link in either pane moves both, and the inspector shows the record's facts.
3. He folds the side navigation away, goes back with the usual key, and opens the shortcut sheet or the command palette when he needs a key he does not know.
