How Jared settles on a look. He presses g then t, arrows through the themes watching the app change, and keeps one with Enter (or Escape to keep what he had). He switches Light, Dark or System from the palette. When no built-in suits him, he opens Appearance from the nav's footer, duplicates the closest theme, adjusts its colors, fonts and density with the app changing around him and warnings where text gets hard to read, then exports it to share.
