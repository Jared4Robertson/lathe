---
id: LA-TRC-DR32
kind: domain_rule
title: Coverage shows every obligation kind the engine counts, and only what it counts
parent: LA-TRC-A1
---
A page that shows coverage MUST show every obligation kind the engine reports, in the engine's order (the meta-model's), and MUST NOT name obligation kinds of its own. A count the engine does not report MUST NOT be shown as zero.
