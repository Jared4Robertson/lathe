---
id: LA-TRC-DR39
kind: domain_rule
title: A waived rule is neither owed nor proved
parent: LA-TRC-A3
---
A pattern rule waived in a domain, because no follower there gives a value that meets its `applies_when`, MUST NOT count in that domain at all: not as owed, designed, built or proved, and not as a gap. It stays owed in each other domain where a follower meets it.
