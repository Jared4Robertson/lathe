The owner asks how complete a domain is: how many of its obligations (requirements, domain rules, interaction requirements and any obligation kind the product adds) are designed (satisfied by a component), built (satisfied by a component whose file exists), proved (by an automated or manual test) and only planned, and which ones are not done. `LA-TRC-UC02` says how the meta-model decides what is counted.
