title: The evidence totals the product's tests and its owed proof
parent: LA-TRC-UC13
---
The product's Evidence panel MUST show a `Tests` row totalling its domains' tests by state, and its `Planned` row MUST add `; N more planned beside proof` when proof is still owed for obligations already proved.
