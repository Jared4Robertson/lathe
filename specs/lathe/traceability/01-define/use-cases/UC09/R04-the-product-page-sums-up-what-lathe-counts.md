id: LA-TRC-UC09-R04
kind: requirement
title: The product page sums up what Lathe counts across the product
parent: LA-TRC-UC09
---
The product page MUST show, in a Summary section, what Lathe counts across the product's domains: its coverage (designed, built, proved and planned), its tests by state, its open questions, and its errors and warnings. A product with nothing to count MUST show zeros, not an empty section.
