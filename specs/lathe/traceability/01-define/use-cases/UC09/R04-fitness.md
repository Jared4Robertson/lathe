---
id: LA-TRC-UC09-R04
kind: requirement
title: The product page shows its fitness apart from the evidence
parent: LA-TRC-UC09
---
The product page MUST show the product's latest `fitness` record (its score from 1 to 5, its date and its body) and the history of all its fitness records, newest first.

Beside it, in a separate Evidence panel, the page MUST show the signals it can count: coverage (designed, built and proved), open questions, errors and warnings. The panel MUST NOT present these as a score, since fitness is only ever the judgment of the person the product serves.

When the product has no fitness record, the page MUST say so and explain how to add one, with a link to the format's fitness section.
