The Reader opens a record and, below its body, reads its Traceability: for a requirement or rule, the components that design it (each marked built or not), the tests that prove it (each with its state), the tests that support it, and the flows, views and endpoints that carry it out, with a callout for each gap coverage finds. A use case shows its requirements in a table of Designed, Built and Proved. A component shows what it satisfies and the tests that prove those things; a test shows what it proves and the file that runs it. Every list is titled by its links' label, read from this record, as are the record facts, the side bar, the graph and the Model view.
