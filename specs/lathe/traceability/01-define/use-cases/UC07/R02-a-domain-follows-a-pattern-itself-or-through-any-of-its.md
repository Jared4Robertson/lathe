id: LA-TRC-UC07-R02
kind: requirement
title: A domain follows a pattern itself or through any of its use cases
parent: LA-TRC-UC07
---
Coverage and the `unsatisfied` and `unproved` checks MUST treat a domain as following a pattern when the domain or any of its use cases follows it, and MUST count each rule once there. When the domain follows only through its use cases, a rule whose setting all of them mark Not applicable MUST NOT be counted.
