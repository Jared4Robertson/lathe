---
id: LA-TRC-UC07-R05
kind: requirement
title: A proof-only obligation is counted for proof alone
parent: LA-TRC-UC07
group: Proof only
---
For a proof-only kind, coverage MUST report designed and built as equal to the total and give no `satisfies` gaps, and the `unsatisfied` warning MUST NOT fire. Proved, planned, planned_any and `proves` gaps MUST work as for any obligation.
