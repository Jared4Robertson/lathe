---
id: LA-TRC-UC02-R01
kind: requirement
title: Coverage counts every obligation kind
parent: LA-TRC-UC02
---
For each domain, coverage MUST give one entry per kind marked `obligation: yes` in the meta-model, in meta-model order, including `interaction_requirement` and kinds added by extension, even when the domain has none of that kind. Each entry MUST give the total and how many are designed, built, proved and planned.
