---
id: LA-TRC-UC14-R04
kind: requirement
title: A component and a test show what they design, prove and run from
parent: LA-TRC-UC14
---
A component's Traceability MUST show Satisfies (what it designs), then Proved through (the tests proving those things, rolled up), then its other links under their labels. A test's MUST show Proves and Supports, then the file that runs it with its state, flagged when the file is missing. Any other kind MUST show its links out under their forward labels and its links in under their reverse labels.
