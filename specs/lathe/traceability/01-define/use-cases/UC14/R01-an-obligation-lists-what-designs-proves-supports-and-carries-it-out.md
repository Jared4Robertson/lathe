---
id: LA-TRC-UC14-R01
kind: requirement
title: An obligation lists what designs, proves, supports and carries it out
parent: LA-TRC-UC14
---
An obligation's record page MUST show a Traceability section after its body and before its children, with these groups in this order, each left out when empty: Designed by (its incoming `design`-role links, each component marked built or not built), Proved by (its incoming `proof`-role links, each test with its state), Supported by, Carried out in, Applied by, then its other incoming links under their reverse labels.
