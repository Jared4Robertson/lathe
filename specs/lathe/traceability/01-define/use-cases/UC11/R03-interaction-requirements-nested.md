---
id: LA-TRC-UC11-R03
kind: requirement
title: Interaction requirements are listed under their view, endpoint or flow
parent: LA-TRC-UC11
---
The domain page MUST list each interaction requirement, and each record of an obligation kind a product adds, nested under the view, endpoint or flow it belongs to, with its gaps and problems on its row. Searching for one MUST keep its view, endpoint or flow shown above it.
