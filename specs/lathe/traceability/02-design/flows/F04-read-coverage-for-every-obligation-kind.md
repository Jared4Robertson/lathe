1. The reader opens a product and then a domain, and reads one coverage group per obligation kind: designed, built, proved and planned.
2. They open a gap, such as an interaction requirement at an endpoint that no test proves yet, on its record page.
3. On the record page each test that proves it shows its state: automated, manual or planned.
