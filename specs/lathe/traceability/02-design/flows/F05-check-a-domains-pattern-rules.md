1. The reader opens a domain page and unfolds **Patterns we follow**: each pattern the domain follows, with each of its rules marked designed, built and proved in this domain, or not applicable with the use case and reason.
2. They open a pattern from there: its page lists its settings, its rules and the domains and use cases that follow it, with each domain's proof status per rule.
3. Reading a rule's text, they follow a cited record ID in the prose to that record; an ID that no longer exists is shown as a broken link.
4. In the Graph view, the pattern's rules sit under the pattern and each following use case's `follows` link is drawn as a cross-link.
