The foundation says a tool's Fitness is the gap between its Purpose and its Condition, assigned by the person it serves from their Experiences of using it. A tool never assigns its own fitness.

For Lathe to improve itself we need a way for Jared to record that judgment. Options to decide between:

- A periodic, hand-written Fitness record (for example a `fitness` kind with a date, a score and the experiences behind it).
- Indirect signals Lathe can count, such as coverage, open questions closed, or time from an edit to a clean `lathe check`, used as evidence but never as the score itself.
- Both: signals as evidence, with Jared's recorded judgment as the Fitness.

This also answers the older open question: if Lathe improves itself, who decides what "better" means. The working answer is Jared, but how that is captured is undecided.
