title: Open Product Folder picks a folder and opens it in its own window
parent: LA-PLT-UC11
---
File › Open Product Folder… (Cmd+O, Ctrl+O elsewhere) MUST let the reader choose a folder and open it in a window of its own. A folder that is not a product MUST be refused with a message saying what is missing.
