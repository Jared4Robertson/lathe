title: The default shortcuts follow VS Code
parent: LA-PLT-UC03
---
The defaults MUST be: Cmd/Ctrl+P and Cmd/Ctrl+K quick open; Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+P the command palette; Cmd/Ctrl+B the nav; Cmd/Ctrl+Alt+B the side bar; Ctrl+W (Alt+W off a Mac) or Ctrl+1, Ctrl+G (Alt+G) or Ctrl+2, and Ctrl+M (Alt+M) or Ctrl+4 the Wiki, Graph and Model views on and off; Ctrl+3 the focused pane alone (full screen) and back, with Escape to leave it; Ctrl+- and Ctrl+Shift+- back and forward (Alt+Left and Alt+Right left to the browser); F12 open the focused link's record; Shift+F12 the incoming links in the side bar; Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+M Checks; F8 and Shift+F8 the next and previous problem on the page; Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+E focus the nav; Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+I or Cmd/Ctrl+Alt+I focus the side bar; `/` the nearest search box; `?` and Cmd/Ctrl+/ the shortcuts sheet; in the graph `+` and `-` depth, `t` trace mode, `.` fit, `o` open or close the focused group, with the arrow keys and Enter as before; and `g` then `c`, `f`, `d` or `h` for Checks, the format, Diagrams and home, with a small hint while the second key is awaited.
