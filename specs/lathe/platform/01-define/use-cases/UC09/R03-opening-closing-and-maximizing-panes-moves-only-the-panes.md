title: Opening, closing and maximizing panes moves only the panes
parent: LA-PLT-UC09
---
Opening or closing the Wiki, Graph or Model pane, the side bar, and full screen (Ctrl+3) MUST change only the panes' widths: the nav stays, a pane that stays open is not drawn anew, and a canvas that opens appears already fitted.
