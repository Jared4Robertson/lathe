---
id: LA-PLT-UC05-R01
kind: requirement
title: A gap between panels resizes them by drag or keyboard
parent: LA-PLT-UC05
---
Each gap between two panels MUST resize them when dragged, and when focused MUST step with the arrow keys (bigger steps with Shift) and jump to the limits with Home and End, the arrow toward a panel growing it. Each size MUST stay within its limits: the navigation 256 to 560 px, the side bar 260 to 640 px, and each pane 20 to 80 percent of the two panes beside a gap, and never narrower than 220 px.
