id: LA-PLT-UC08-R02
kind: requirement
title: A phone shows one view at a time, switched from the page bar
parent: LA-PLT-UC08
---
On a phone a record, group, product or domain page MUST show one view at a time, full width, with no pane headers and no resize handles: the first of the views the address names. The page bar MUST sit under the top bar, compact: the Open pages button, the View switch (Wiki, Graph and Model where offered, one pressed) and the side bar toggle. Switching MUST show that view alone, name it in the address (`?view=graph`) and keep it in the tab, and MUST leave the views remembered for a wide screen alone.
