title: Quick find and the command palette fill the screen
parent: LA-PLT-UC08
group: Sheets
---
On a phone quick find MUST open from the top bar (and from its shortcut) as a full-screen sheet: the search box at the top with a Close button, the results as a list under it, more of them than in the dropdown. Choosing a result MUST go there and close the sheet; Escape or Close MUST close it and give focus back. The command palette and the shortcuts sheet MUST fill the screen too.
