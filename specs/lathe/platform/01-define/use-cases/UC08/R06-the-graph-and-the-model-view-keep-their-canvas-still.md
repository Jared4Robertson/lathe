kind: requirement
title: The graph and the Model view keep their canvas still
parent: LA-PLT-UC08
---
Re-centering the graph at any depth, changing the depth, and moving between a domain's, an aggregate's and an entity's ERD MUST keep the pane title, the header and the canvas at one size and place; only the boxes and the camera move, on the shared motion.
