kind: requirement
title: A tablet in landscape keeps the workbench, sized for touch
parent: LA-PLT-UC08
group: Layout
---
The screen's width MUST decide the layout and the pointer the size of targets: at 900 px and wider, a tablet MUST keep the workbench (nav, panes, side bar), with every target at least 44 × 44 px on a touch screen (`LA-PAT-APP-UX-R07`); below 900 px, a tablet in portrait MUST take the phone layout.
