---
id: LA-PLT-UC08-R05
kind: requirement
title: The open pages are a sheet
parent: LA-PLT-UC08
---
On a phone the tabs MUST fold into an Open pages button in the page bar that shows how many are open and opens them as a bottom sheet: one row per tab with its ID and title, the active one marked, the preview's title in italics with a Keep button, and a close button on every row. Choosing a row MUST go to that page and close the sheet.
