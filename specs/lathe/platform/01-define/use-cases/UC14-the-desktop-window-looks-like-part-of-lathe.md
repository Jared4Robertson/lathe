actor: Reader

The reader works in a window with no system title bar, as in VS Code: the window's controls float over Lathe's own bar, drawn in the theme's colors. From the bar they go back and forward, see which page they are on and open quick find, and show or hide the navigation and the side bar. They drag the window by the bar and double-click it to zoom. When they change the theme or the light or dark mode, the bar and the window's controls follow.
