---
id: LA-PLT-UC04-R01
kind: requirement
title: A tooltip shows the control and its keys
parent: LA-PLT-UC04
---
Every icon button and control that has a shortcut MUST show it in its tooltip as its name then its keys ("Show or hide the navigation ⌘B", or "Ctrl+B" off a Mac), after a short hover delay and at once on keyboard focus.
