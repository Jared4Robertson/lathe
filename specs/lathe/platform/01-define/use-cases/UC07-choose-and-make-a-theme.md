The reader picks how Lathe looks, as in VS Code: one of a few built-in themes (Lathe, High contrast, Workshop, Midnight and Graphite), and Light, Dark or the system's choice. They arrow through the themes and see each one at once. When none suits them, they duplicate one and change its colors, fonts, text size, density, corners, gaps and motion, with a warning wherever text gets hard to read, and share the result as a file.
