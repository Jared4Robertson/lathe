---
id: LA-PLT-UC06-R03
kind: requirement
title: The Appearance page lists the themes with previews
parent: LA-PLT-UC06
---
`/settings/appearance` MUST list every theme, built in and the reader's own, each with a small preview of the app in its colors and fonts, the one in use marked, and buttons to use it, to duplicate and edit it (built in) or edit it (his own), and to export it. The page MUST be linked from the nav's footer and reachable from the command palette ("Open Appearance settings").
