---
id: LA-PLT-UC06-R02
kind: requirement
title: Light, dark or system is chosen in the app
parent: LA-PLT-UC06
---
Every theme MUST show its light or dark set by the person's choice of System (the default, following `prefers-color-scheme`), Light or Dark. The choice MUST be made by the "Light, dark or system" command (`chooseColorMode`), which previews like the theme picker, and on the Appearance page.
