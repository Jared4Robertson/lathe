id: LA-PLT-UC06-R03
kind: requirement
title: Dragging the navigation or the side bar small folds it
parent: LA-PLT-UC06
---
Dragging the navigation below 180 px MUST fold it to its rail, and dragging the side bar below 160 px MUST hide it; dragging the rail's gap out again MUST unfold the navigation. On a narrow screen there MUST be no gaps to drag: the navigation and the side bar are sheets.
