---
id: LA-PLT-UC06-R04
kind: requirement
title: A copy of a theme can be edited, renamed, reset and deleted
parent: LA-PLT-UC06
---
"Duplicate and edit" MUST make a copy and open it in an editor with: color pickers grouped by purpose (surfaces, text, borders, accent, signals, graph and depth), the light and dark sets side by side; fonts for headings, text and code from a short safe list or a custom font stack; text size, density (comfortable or compact), panel corner radius, gap between panels, and motion (follow the system or always reduced). The theme being edited MUST be the one in use, so the app around the editor is its live preview, beside small light and dark previews. The editor MUST rename the theme, reset it to the built-in it was copied from, and delete it after asking.
