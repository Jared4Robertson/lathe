---
id: LA-PLT-UC06-R05
kind: requirement
title: Hard-to-read colors are flagged while editing
parent: LA-PLT-UC06
---
While editing, each text or control color whose contrast on what it is drawn on falls below WCAG AA (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text and controls) MUST show a warning beside its swatch, with the ratio, the background and the ratio it needs, for the mode it fails in.
