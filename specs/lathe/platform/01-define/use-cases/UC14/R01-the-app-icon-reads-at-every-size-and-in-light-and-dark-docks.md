---
id: LA-PLT-UC14-R01
kind: requirement
title: The app icon reads at every size and in light and dark Docks
parent: LA-PLT-UC14
---
The app MUST have an icon drawn on Apple's icon grid, rendered from SVG for macOS (`.icns`, 16 to 1024 px), Windows (`.ico`) and Linux (PNGs), with a bolder drawing at 16 and 32 px. A development build (`npm run dev`) MUST show the icon with a "DEV" band in the Dock.
