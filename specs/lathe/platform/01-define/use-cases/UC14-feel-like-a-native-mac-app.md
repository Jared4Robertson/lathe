The Owner keeps Lathe in the Dock like any well-made Mac app. Its icon reads at every size, in light and dark Docks, and a development build is marked DEV. The Dock badge counts the check errors of the product in front, and the Dock menu opens another product folder. About Lathe says which versions run, and Settings… opens the app's settings. From a record the Owner reveals its spec file in Finder, opens it in the editor or copies its ID, from the side bar or a right-click. Opening Lathe again, or a folder from Finder, goes to the running app. A window opens calmly on the shell color, says which product it is opening, and, when the engine cannot load the folder, says why and offers a way on.
