title: The graph and the ERD pan, pinch and take taps
parent: LA-PLT-UC07
---
The graph and the ERD MUST pan with a finger and zoom with a pinch. On a touch screen a first tap on a node MUST select it: the node is outlined and a card at the bottom of the canvas shows its kind and name with an Open chip; a second tap on it, or the chip, MUST open it; a tap on the empty canvas MUST let it go. A click with a mouse MUST still open a node at once. A long press does nothing of its own: the tap's card is the preview.
