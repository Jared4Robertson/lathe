id: LA-PLT-UC10-R02
kind: requirement
title: Every control is reached and worked by the keyboard
parent: LA-PLT-UC10
---
Tab and Shift+Tab MUST reach every control a page draws, each with a visible focus ring, and go all the way round the page and back without being held anywhere. Inside a group of controls (the tab strip, the graph's boxes, a tree's rows) the arrow keys move and Enter or Space acts. The main tasks MUST work by keyboard alone: open a record from the nav or from quick find, switch views, re-center the graph, fold and unfold a row, show, hide and focus the side bar, and pick a theme.
