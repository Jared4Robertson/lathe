---
id: LA-PLT-UC10-R03
kind: requirement
title: Focus lands where the Reader expects
parent: LA-PLT-UC10
---
After a move to another page, focus MUST stay on the page, never fall to the body. A control that changes the page in place (a view toggle, the side bar toggle, a fold button) MUST keep focus. Re-centering the graph by keyboard MUST put focus on the box that became the center. A dialog (quick find, the palette, the shortcuts sheet, the theme picker) or a phone sheet MUST take focus when it opens, keep Tab inside it while it is open, and give focus back to the control that opened it when it closes.
