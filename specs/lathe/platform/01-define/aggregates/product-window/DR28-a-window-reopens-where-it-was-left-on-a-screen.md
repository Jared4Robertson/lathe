---
id: LA-PLT-DR28
kind: domain_rule
title: A window reopens where it was left, on a screen
parent: LA-PLT-A6
---
Each folder's window size, position, and whether it was maximized or full screen MUST be saved when it moves, resizes or closes, and its next window MUST open with them. A saved place that is no longer on a connected screen MUST give way to a centered window, and a size larger than the largest screen MUST shrink to fit it.
