---
id: LA-PLT-DR06
kind: domain_rule
title: The browser's essential keys stay the browser's
parent: LA-PLT-A3
---
No binding, default or overridden, MUST take Cmd/Ctrl+W, T, L, R, N or Q, Tab, F5 or F11, and the app MUST call preventDefault only for keys it handles, so Alt+Left and Alt+Right still go back and forward.
