One control on a page: a link, button, tab, toggle, field or a box that scrolls on its own.

## Fields

| Field | Meaning | Kind of value | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Role | What sort of control it is. | An ARIA role. | button |
| Name | What a screen reader calls it, holding its visible text. | Text. | Open in graph |
| Target | The size it can be hit at. | Pixels, at least 24 or with room around it. | 28 × 28 |
| Keys | The shortcut that runs it, if any. | Key chords. | Alt+G |
