id: LA-PLT-DR03
kind: domain_rule
title: Hiding a panel gives its space away and loses nothing
parent: LA-PLT-A2
---
Hiding the nav or the side bar MUST give its space to the main view and MUST keep a control that brings it back: the nav keeps its rail, and the side bar's toggle sits in the page panel's top-right corner, as VS Code's layout toggles do, never as a full-height strip. The side bar MUST describe the same page whichever view is open, so hiding a pane never hides the record's facts for good.
