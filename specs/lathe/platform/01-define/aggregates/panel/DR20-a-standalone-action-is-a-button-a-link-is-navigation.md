---
id: LA-PLT-DR20
kind: domain_rule
title: A standalone action is a button; a link is navigation in context
parent: LA-PLT-A2
---
A control that stands on its own and does something, or takes the Reader somewhere as a step of its own (opening a product's overview, clearing the graph's filters, expanding every row, cancelling a form), MUST look like a button: a ghost button, or the primary button for the one main action of a form. A link MUST look like a link only where it is navigation in context: inside a sentence or Markdown, in a list or a row, in a breadcrumb or trail, and in the navigation. An action that goes to an address MUST still be a real link, so it can be opened in a new tab.
