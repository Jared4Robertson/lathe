---
id: LA-PLT-DR17
kind: domain_rule
title: A sheet keeps focus until it closes, then gives it back
parent: LA-PLT-A2
---
A sheet (the nav, the side bar, the open pages, quick find) MUST take focus as it opens, keep Tab and Shift+Tab inside it while it is open, close with Escape, and give focus back to the control that opened it.
