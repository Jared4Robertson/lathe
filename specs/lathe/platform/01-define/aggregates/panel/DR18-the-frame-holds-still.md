---
id: LA-PLT-DR18
kind: domain_rule
title: The frame of a page holds still
parent: LA-PLT-A2
---
The nav, the pane bars, the pane titles, the canvases and the side bar MUST keep their place and size unless the Reader moves or resizes them; a change of page changes what they show, not where they are. Content that arrives after the page is drawn (the specs, the diagrams, the fonts, what the graph tells the side bar) MUST take room kept for it, arrive before paint, or be drawn where it pushes nothing the Reader can see.
