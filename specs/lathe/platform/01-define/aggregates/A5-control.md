One thing on a page the Reader acts on, by pointer, by keyboard or through a screen reader: a link, a button, a tab, a toggle, a field, or a box that scrolls on its own. A control has a name that holds what it shows, a target big enough to hit, a place in the keyboard's order, and never sits inside another control.
