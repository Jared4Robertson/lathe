How the reader reads a domain on a phone between meetings. They tap the search button in the top bar, type a use case's ID and tap the result. The wiki fills the screen under the title; they read its requirements, then tap Graph in the page bar. They pinch out, tap a component to see its name, and tap Open. They pull up the side bar to check its coverage gaps, drag it to full height, then swipe it away. The link at the top takes them up to the domain; the Pages button brings back the use case they started from.
