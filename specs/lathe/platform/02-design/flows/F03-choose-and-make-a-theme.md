How the reader settles on a look. They press g then t, arrow through the themes watching the app change, and keep one with Enter (or Escape to keep what they had). They switch Light, Dark or System from the palette. When no built-in suits them, they open Appearance from the nav's footer, duplicate the closest theme, adjust its colors, fonts and density with the app changing around them and warnings where text gets hard to read, then export it to share.
