id: LA-PAT-APP-CODE-R11
kind: pattern_rule
title: Each pane has one title bar, the leftmost holding the tabs and the others naming their view; the rightmost holds the view toggles and the inspector toggle
parent: LA-PAT-APP-CODE
---
A pane that shows content (the wiki, the graph, the model) MUST be a panel whose first child is its one title bar (`pane-bar`), as an editor group's in VS Code. At the left of the leftmost pane, the tab strip of open pages (`role="tablist"`); at the left of every other pane, a quiet label that is not a control names what the pane shows (its icon and "Wiki", "Graph" or "Model"), followed by the button that closes the pane. At its right (`pane-actions`), all as icon buttons in one style (`icon-button`): on the rightmost pane only, the view toggles, a group labeled "View" whose buttons carry `aria-pressed`, then a thin divider; on the leftmost pane, when more than one pane is open, its close button; on every pane its full screen button; and on the rightmost pane the inspector toggle, on a narrow screen too, where it opens the inspector drawer. So the View toggles and the inspector toggle appear once on the page. A toggle that is on, an open view or a shown inspector, MUST use the one shared on look (`icon-button is-on`). View switches and layout actions MUST NOT appear anywhere else on the page, such as a header band above the panes, a row of their own on a narrow screen, or a button floating over a pane. The page's title belongs to the content: it sits at the top of the leftmost open pane.
