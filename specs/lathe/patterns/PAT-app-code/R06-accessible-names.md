id: LA-PAT-APP-CODE-R06
kind: pattern_rule
title: Every control has an accessible name
parent: LA-PAT-APP-CODE
---
Every button, link and form control MUST have an accessible name: its text, an `aria-label`, or a label. An icon-only button names what it does ("Collapse LA-BRW-UC01"), and a control with a keyboard shortcut also states it in `aria-keyshortcuts`.
