Running the checks and reporting what they find: each check is one SQL file, a problem names its check and its record, and an error means the workspace is broken while a warning is work still to do. A product adds checks of its own. Checking also keeps the structure checks that find gaps, stale citations and levels of the spec that are the wrong size, and shows every problem on the Checks page and the Dock badge.
