id: LA-CHK-UC25-R03
kind: requirement
title: A product's reference shows its extensions and its own checks
parent: LA-CHK-UC25
---
The reference MUST be the product's own: its kinds, relations and folders after its `metaExtensions` rows, and its own checks after Lathe's and the engine's. A product check that does not run, being named like one of Lathe's, MUST NOT be listed.
