---
id: LA-CHK-UC23-R04
kind: requirement
title: A lone child counts the record's own children of every kind
parent: LA-CHK-UC23
---
The `lone-child` check MUST count every child of the capability or layer, of any kind, and only its own children, not theirs. A capability or layer with no children, or with two or more, MUST NOT be reported.
