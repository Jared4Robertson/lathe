title: Grouped children are counted per group
parent: LA-CHK-UC06
---
`too-many-children` MUST count requirements, interaction requirements, pattern rules and standard rules per `group`, the ungrouped ones as one list, with groups compared trimmed and in any letter case, since each group reads under its own heading; its message MUST name the group.
