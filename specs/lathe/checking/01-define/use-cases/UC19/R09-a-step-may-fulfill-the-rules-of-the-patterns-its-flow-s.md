---
id: LA-CHK-UC19-R09
kind: requirement
title: A step may fulfill rules of the patterns its flow's use cases or domain follow
parent: LA-CHK-UC19
---
`flow-step-fulfills-outside-flow` MUST treat as inside a flow the rules of the patterns that a use case the flow carries out, or the flow's domain, follows, and the interaction requirements that fulfill them; a step that fulfills any other pattern rule, or an interaction requirement that fulfills one, MUST be reported.
