---
id: LA-CHK-UC13-R03
kind: requirement
title: Only record IDs and IDs with a known prefix are citations
parent: LA-CHK-UC13
---
An ID-shaped token MUST count as a citation only when it stands as a whole ID and either is the ID of a record or starts with a known prefix followed by a hyphen, as LA-CHK-UC18 sets out. Other capitalised words, and IDs with a prefix the workspace does not know, MUST be ignored.
