---
id: LA-CHK-UC13-R03
kind: requirement
title: Only IDs with a product prefix of the workspace are citations
parent: LA-CHK-UC13
---
An ID-shaped token MUST count as a citation only when it equals the ID of a product record in the workspace or starts with one followed by a hyphen, and only when it stands as a whole ID. Other capitalised words and IDs of other products MUST be ignored.
