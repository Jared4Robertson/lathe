---
id: LA-CHK-UC07-R01
kind: requirement
title: A space's Checks page lists its product's problems, errors first, grouped by check
parent: LA-CHK-UC07
---
A space's Checks page MUST list every problem of its product, errors before warnings, grouped by check, each linking to its record, then the problems that belong to no product (a broken meta-model or config) under a Workspace heading, which every space's Checks page shows. It MUST NOT list another product's problems.
