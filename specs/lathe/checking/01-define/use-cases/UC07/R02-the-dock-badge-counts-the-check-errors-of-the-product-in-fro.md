id: LA-CHK-UC07-R02
kind: requirement
title: The Dock badge counts the check errors of the product in front
parent: LA-CHK-UC07
---
The Dock badge MUST show the number of check errors of the focused window's current space, its product's and the workspace's (as its Checks page counts them), follow it when the specs reload or the space changes, and show nothing when there are none.
