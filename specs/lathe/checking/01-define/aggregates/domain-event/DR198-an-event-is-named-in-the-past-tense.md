id: LA-CHK-DR198
kind: domain_rule
title: An event is named in the past tense
parent: LA-CHK-A13
---
A domain event's title MUST say what happened, in the past tense and subject first, such as Loan opened or Loan became overdue. Since only a heuristic can tell, a title with no past form as its last word or after its first SHOULD be reported as a warning, not an error.
