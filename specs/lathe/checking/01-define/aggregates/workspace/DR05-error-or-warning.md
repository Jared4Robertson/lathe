---
id: LA-CHK-DR05
kind: domain_rule
title: "Errors mean broken; warnings mean work to do"
parent: LA-CHK-A2
---
A problem MUST be an **error** when the workspace is broken (a record rule in `LA-MOD-A2` is not met, a file cannot be parsed, a fitness record's date or score is not valid, or the meta-model is not consistent) and a **warning** when work is still to do (a requirement or rule not yet satisfied or proved, a use case with no requirements, a component file that does not exist yet).
