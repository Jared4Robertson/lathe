---
id: LA-CHK-DR70
kind: domain_rule
title: "A citation is a whole ID with a product prefix of the workspace"
parent: LA-CHK-A2
---
A citation MUST be an ID-shaped token, read whole, that equals a product's ID or starts with it followed by a hyphen. A token is never split: the tail of `X-TL-LEND-DR05` is not a citation of `TL-LEND-DR05`, and `TL-LEND-DR05a` cites nothing.
