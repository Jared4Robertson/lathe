---
id: LA-CHK-DR135
kind: domain_rule
title: A proof-only obligation is never asked for design
parent: LA-CHK-A2
---
An obligation of a proof-only kind MUST count as designed and built in every domain that owes it, MUST have no `satisfies` gap and MUST NOT be reported `unsatisfied`. Its proof MUST be counted, planned and reported as for any other obligation.
