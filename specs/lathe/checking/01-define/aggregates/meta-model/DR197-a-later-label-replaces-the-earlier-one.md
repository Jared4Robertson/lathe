---
id: LA-CHK-DR197
kind: domain_rule
title: A later non-empty label replaces the earlier one
parent: LA-CHK-A1
---
A row in a later folder that restates a relation rule (`LA-CHK-DR81`) MUST replace the rule's `forward_label` or `reverse_label` with its own when that is non-empty, and MUST keep the earlier label when it is empty, as for the role and description.
