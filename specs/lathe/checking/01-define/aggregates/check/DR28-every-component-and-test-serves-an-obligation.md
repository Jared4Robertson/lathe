---
id: LA-CHK-DR28
kind: domain_rule
title: Every component and test serves an obligation
parent: LA-CHK-A1
---
A component MUST satisfy at least one obligation, and a test MUST prove or support at least one, so that nothing in the design outlives its purpose unseen. One that does not MUST be reported as a warning. No component or test is waived: a component of shared infrastructure links the obligations it makes possible.
