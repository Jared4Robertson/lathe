---
id: LA-CHK-DR01
kind: domain_rule
title: Errors mean broken; warnings mean work to do
parent: LA-CHK-EN02
---
A problem MUST be an **error** when the workspace is broken (a record rule in `LA-AUT-A1` is not met, a file cannot be parsed, or the meta-model is not consistent) and a **warning** when work is still to do (an obligation not yet satisfied or proved, a use case with no requirements, a component file that does not exist yet).
