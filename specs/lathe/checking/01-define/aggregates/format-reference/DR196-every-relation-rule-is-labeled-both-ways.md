---
id: LA-CHK-DR196
kind: domain_rule
title: Every relation rule is labeled both ways
parent: LA-CHK-A12
---
Every relation rule, Lathe's or an extension's, MUST have a non-empty `forward_label` and `reverse_label` after the product's extensions are merged. Every place that shows a link, in the wiki, the inspector, the graph and the reference, reads it by these words rather than by the relation's name.
