---
id: LA-CHK-DR70
kind: domain_rule
title: A citation is read as a whole ID
parent: LA-CHK-A8
---
A citation MUST be an ID-shaped token read whole, and is one only under LA-CHK-DR120 to LA-CHK-DR122. A token is never split: the tail of `X-TL-LEND-DR05` is not a citation of `TL-LEND-DR05`, and `TL-LEND-DR05a` cites nothing.
