---
id: LA-MOD-DR01
kind: domain_rule
title: A diagram slug is unique and URL-safe
parent: LA-MOD-A1
---
A diagram's slug MUST be unique among the diagrams in `model/diagrams.csv`, and MUST contain only lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. The slug is both the diagram's folder name and the last part of its address, `/diagrams/<slug>`.
