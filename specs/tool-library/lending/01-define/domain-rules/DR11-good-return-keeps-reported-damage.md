---
id: TL-LEND-DR11
kind: domain_rule
title: A good return does not clear damage reported during the loan
parent: TL-LEND-A2
---
When damage is reported on a tool while it is on loan, a return that records the condition as `good` MUST NOT clear that damage. The tool stays `needs_repair`, with its damage note, until a librarian marks it repaired.
