Sends `POST /tools/{toolId}/damage` with a body of exactly 64 KB and expects it to be read and the note saved. Sends one a byte over and expects 413 `body_too_large` with `Connection: close`, and the tool still `good`. Sends an over-limit body to `POST /members` and expects the same 413, so the limit holds on every endpoint's body, and the email is still free afterwards.

Then, over a raw socket, it sends a chunked damage report with no declared length that never ends, writing as fast as the connection allows. It expects a 413 `body_too_large` answer with `Connection: close`, and the server to close the connection within 5 seconds instead of waiting for the body to end.
