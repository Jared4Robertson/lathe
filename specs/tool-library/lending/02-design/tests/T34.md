Proves the Librarian action pattern's second rule in Lending, for each use case that follows it. A registered member is shown with its ID and stored name and email, and its email cannot register again. An added tool, and a tool after damage is reported, are shown exactly as the catalog then lists them, which also proves the third rule, owed here because adding a tool and reporting damage save a Tool. A returned loan is shown with its condition and note, cannot be returned again, and the tool's catalog entry reflects it.
