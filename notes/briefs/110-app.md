## Goal
The app reads, shows and edits every product in the new model, with the same look and quality bar as today. Use the converted Tool Library (`examples/tool-library/`) as the real product while you work; LA is converted at the same time.

## Decisions (Owner)
- **Stages and colors:** kinds group and order by the five stages (domain model, use cases, interaction design, technical specification, test plan) from the schema description, with one color per stage used consistently (graph, badges, side bar). Product and role sit outside the stages.
- **Pages** for every kind, with its typed fields, its parts (fields with their types, value objects and aggregate references; table keys; a command's recorded exceptions; a flow's steps; a test's run by), its parent and its links in both directions.
- **Edit, add and remove** keep working from the schema description: a parent picker that offers both kinds where a record may sit under either; no parent picker for kinds with no owner (their product and domain come from the ID); part rows editable in place.
- **The graph and Model view** draw the new tree. The Model view shows a domain's aggregates with their entities, value objects and references, and the commands, events, policies, specifications, read models and queries around them, in event storming colors.
- **Checks and coverage** show what the engine reports. The engine brief (108) owns the engine and app contract (`lathe/src/types.ts`, `lathe/src/server/contract.ts`); build on the store and schema description first, then merge `domain-model` once the engine branch lands and adopt its contract. Don't edit the contract.
- **Fixtures:** convert the app's test fixtures to the new model.
